*Pseudomonas tolaasii* is a Gram-negative, aerobic rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, primarily known as the causal agent of brown blotch disease of cultivated mushrooms, particularly *Agaricus bisporus*. It produces the lipodepsipeptide toxin tolaasin, which disrupts mushroom cell membranes and causes the characteristic brown, sunken lesions on mushroom caps. *P. tolaasii* is closely related to other fluorescent pseudomonads, and its genome encodes non-ribosomal peptide synthetases responsible for tolaasin biosynthesis.
