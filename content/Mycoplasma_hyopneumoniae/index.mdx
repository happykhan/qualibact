*Mycoplasma hyopneumoniae* is a cell-wall-lacking, Gram-indeterminate bacterium in the family *Mycoplasmataceae* (class *Mollicutes*), and the primary agent of enzootic pneumonia in pigs, one of the most economically significant diseases in the global swine industry. It adheres to the ciliated respiratory epithelium causing chronic coughing, reduced growth rates, and predisposition to secondary bacterial infections. The genome of *M. hyopneumoniae* (~0.9 Mb) encodes a complex surface proteome with numerous adhesins and paralogous gene families involved in host interaction and antigenic variation, despite its minimal coding capacity.
