*Xanthomonas fragariae* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped bacterium of the family *Xanthomonadaceae* and the sole causal agent of angular leaf spot of strawberry, a quarantine-listed disease in many countries. It enters leaves through stomata, producing water-soaked angular lesions that reduce photosynthetic area and fruit yield. *X. fragariae* is notable among xanthomonads for its narrow host range restricted to *Fragaria* species, and its genome encodes a reduced type III effector complement compared with broader-host-range relatives, reflecting a specialised pathogenic lifestyle.
