*Shewanella algae* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic, motile rod in the family *Shewanellaceae*, commonly found in marine and estuarine environments and associated with marine organisms. It is the most clinically relevant *Shewanella* species, causing wound infections, ear infections, bacteraemia, and necrotising fasciitis, typically following exposure to warm seawater, particularly in individuals with hepatic disease or immunosuppression. *S. algae* is notable for its ability to use a wide range of terminal electron acceptors during anaerobic respiration, and its genome encodes multiple cytochrome systems that facilitate metal reduction.
