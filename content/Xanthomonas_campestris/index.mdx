*Xanthomonas campestris* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped bacterium of the family *Xanthomonadaceae* and a globally significant plant pathogen, best known as the causal agent of black rot in cruciferous crops. It produces the exopolysaccharide xanthan gum, which has widespread industrial applications in food and drilling fluids. *X. campestris* genomes encode a sophisticated type III secretion system delivering diverse effector proteins into host cells, and comparative genomics has revealed extensive pathovar-level diversity reflecting adaptation to different Brassicaceae hosts.
