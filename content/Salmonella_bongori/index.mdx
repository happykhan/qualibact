*Salmonella bongori* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, and one of the two recognised species in the genus *Salmonella*. It is primarily associated with cold-blooded animals such as reptiles and is a rare cause of gastroenteritis in humans, particularly in young children and immunocompromised individuals. *S. bongori* lacks the Salmonella pathogenicity island 2 (SPI-2) type III secretion system that is central to systemic virulence in *S. enterica*, providing important evolutionary insight into the acquisition of intracellular parasitism within the genus.
