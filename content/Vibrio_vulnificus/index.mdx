*Vibrio vulnificus* is a Gram-negative, curved rod of the family *Vibrionaceae* found in warm, brackish coastal waters, notorious for causing rapidly fatal septicaemia and necrotising wound infections in immunocompromised individuals. It has one of the highest case-fatality rates of any foodborne pathogen, particularly following raw oyster consumption or wound exposure to seawater. *V. vulnificus* genomes harbour a range of virulence factors including a polysaccharide capsule, MARTX toxin, and iron-acquisition systems, with biotype and lineage classification reflecting distinct clinical and ecological niches.
