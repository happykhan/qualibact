*Dichelobacter nodosus* is an anaerobic, Gram-negative rod of the family *Cardiobacteriaceae* and the essential causative agent of ovine footrot, an economically significant lameness disease of sheep worldwide. Virulent strains produce thermostable extracellular proteases (AprV2) and type IV fimbriae that mediate host tissue invasion and are the basis for serogroup classification. The genome of *D. nodosus* is relatively small (~1.4 Mb), reflects its obligate host association, and contains a large integrated genomic island (vap region) implicated in virulence.
