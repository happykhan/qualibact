*Riemerella anatipestifer* is a Gram-negative, non-motile, non-spore-forming rod in the family *Weeksellaceae* (phylum *Bacteroidota*), and a major pathogen of domestic ducks, geese, and turkeys worldwide. It causes riemerellosis (infectious serositis), characterised by fibrinous pericarditis, perihepatitis, and airsacculitis, leading to significant mortality and economic losses in the poultry industry. The genome of *R. anatipestifer* encodes gliding motility machinery, polysaccharide capsule loci, and a type IX secretion system, with high serotypic diversity (at least 21 serotypes) complicating vaccine development.
