*Aeromonas veronii* is a facultatively anaerobic, oxidase-positive Gram-negative rod in the family *Aeromonadaceae*, widely distributed in aquatic environments and the gastrointestinal tracts of fish and leeches. It is clinically significant as a cause of gastroenteritis, wound infections, and bacteraemia in humans, and also causes motile aeromonad septicaemia in farmed fish. *A. veronii* harbours aerolysin, type II and type VI secretion systems, and a chromosomal metallo-beta-lactamase, and genomic studies reveal substantial intraspecific diversity with a large accessory genome.
