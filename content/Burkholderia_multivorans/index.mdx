*Burkholderia multivorans* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting rod of the family *Burkholderiaceae* and a member of the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc). It is one of the most frequently isolated Bcc species from the respiratory tract of cystic fibrosis patients and can cause chronic lung infections with variable clinical outcomes. *B. multivorans* has a multi-replicon genome with considerable strain-level diversity, an array of virulence-associated genes, and intrinsic resistance to aminoglycosides and polymyxins.
