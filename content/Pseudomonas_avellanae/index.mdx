*Pseudomonas avellanae* is a Gram-negative, non-spore-forming rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae* and a member of the *Pseudomonas syringae* species complex. It is the causal agent of bacterial canker and decline of hazelnut (*Corylus avellana*), a disease of major economic concern in Mediterranean nut-growing regions. *P. avellanae* harbours a type III secretion system and effector repertoire similar to other *P. syringae* pathovars, though with a more restricted host range and a relatively compact genome.
