*Xanthomonas oryzae* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped bacterium of the family *Xanthomonadaceae* and the causal agent of bacterial blight (pv. *oryzae*) and bacterial leaf streak (pv. *oryzicola*) of rice, two of the most destructive diseases of this staple crop. It is prevalent in rice-growing regions across Asia and Africa and causes substantial yield losses. *X. oryzae* genomes are notable for large numbers of transcription activator-like (TAL) effector genes, insertion sequences, and extensive structural variation that drive virulence adaptation and the arms race with host resistance genes.
