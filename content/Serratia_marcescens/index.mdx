*Serratia marcescens* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Yersiniaceae*, well known for producing the red pigment prodigiosin and widely found in soil, water, and hospital environments. It is a clinically significant opportunistic pathogen responsible for nosocomial outbreaks of bloodstream infections, respiratory infections, urinary tract infections, and meningitis, particularly in neonatal intensive care units. *S. marcescens* possesses intrinsic resistance to colistin and several beta-lactams via chromosomal AmpC, and its genome encodes diverse secretion systems, biofilm determinants, and a broad array of acquired antimicrobial resistance genes.
