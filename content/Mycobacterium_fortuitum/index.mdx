*Mycobacterium fortuitum* is a rapidly growing, acid-fast bacillus in the family *Mycobacteriaceae*, ubiquitous in soil, water, and dust. It is an opportunistic pathogen that causes post-surgical wound infections, catheter-related bloodstream infections, and skin and soft-tissue disease, particularly following penetrating trauma or cosmetic procedures. The genome of *M. fortuitum* (~6.3 Mb) is among the largest in the rapidly growing mycobacteria and encodes multiple intrinsic antibiotic resistance determinants, though it notably lacks a functional *erm* gene, rendering it susceptible to macrolides.
