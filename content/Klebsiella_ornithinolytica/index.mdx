*Klebsiella ornithinolytica* (reclassified as *Raoultella ornithinolytica*) is a Gram-negative, encapsulated rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, characterised by its ability to decarboxylate ornithine. It is found in aquatic environments, soil, and fish, and is an emerging opportunistic pathogen that can cause bacteraemia, urinary tract infections, and biliary sepsis, particularly in immunocompromised patients. The species possesses a chromosomally encoded class A beta-lactamase and shows notable genomic diversity in its accessory genome.
