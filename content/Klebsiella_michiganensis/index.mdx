*Klebsiella michiganensis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, belonging to the *Klebsiella oxytoca* species complex and initially isolated from a toothbrush holder. It is found in clinical, environmental, and food-associated samples and can cause opportunistic infections similar to those of *K. oxytoca*, including bacteraemia and urinary tract infections. *K. michiganensis* frequently harbours the cytotoxin biosynthetic gene cluster and can carry extended-spectrum beta-lactamase and carbapenemase genes on mobile genetic elements.
