*Bordetella parapertussis* is a Gram-negative, aerobic coccobacillus of the family *Alcaligenaceae* that causes a pertussis-like respiratory illness in humans, generally milder than that caused by *B. pertussis*. A distinct ovine-adapted lineage also circulates in sheep, causing chronic non-progressive pneumonia. *B. parapertussis* evolved from a *B. bronchiseptica*-like ancestor through independent genome reduction, notably losing the pertussis toxin promoter while retaining the structural genes, and carries abundant IS elements that have driven extensive genomic rearrangement.
