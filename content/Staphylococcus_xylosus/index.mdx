*Staphylococcus xylosus* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, commonly found on the skin of mammals and in food environments. It is widely used as a starter culture in fermented meat and dairy products, where it contributes to colour stability and flavour through nitrate reduction and lipid metabolism. *S. xylosus* has a genome of approximately 2.8 Mb, is generally non-pathogenic, but occasional strains carry antimicrobial resistance genes and have been linked to opportunistic infections in immunocompromised hosts.
