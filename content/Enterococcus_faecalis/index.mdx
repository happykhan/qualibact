*Enterococcus faecalis* is a Gram-positive, catalase-negative coccus in the family *Enterococcaceae*, and a normal inhabitant of the human gastrointestinal tract. It is a leading cause of healthcare-associated infections including endocarditis, urinary tract infections, bacteraemia, and intra-abdominal and wound infections. *E. faecalis* possesses a versatile genome rich in mobile genetic elements, pheromone-responsive plasmids, and pathogenicity islands encoding virulence factors such as cytolysin, gelatinase, and aggregation substance.
