*Limosilactobacillus fermentum* (formerly *Lactobacillus fermentum*) is an obligately heterofermentative, Gram-positive rod in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, widespread in fermented foods, sourdough, and the human gastrointestinal and urogenital tracts. It is investigated as a probiotic for its cholesterol-lowering, antioxidant, and immunomodulatory properties. The genome of *L. fermentum* (~2.1 Mb) encodes diverse sugar transport and phosphoketolase pathway genes that support its heterofermentative metabolism.
