*Borreliella afzelii* is a Gram-negative spirochaete of the family *Borreliaceae* and one of the principal agents of Lyme borreliosis in Europe. Transmitted by *Ixodes* ticks, it has a particular tropism for skin tissue and is the main cause of acrodermatitis chronica atrophicans, a late cutaneous manifestation of Lyme disease. *B. afzelii* possesses a highly segmented linear genome with numerous linear and circular plasmids that encode surface lipoproteins critical for immune evasion and host adaptation.
