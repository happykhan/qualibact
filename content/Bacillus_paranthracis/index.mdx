*Bacillus paranthracis* is an aerobic, endospore-forming Gram-positive rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, belonging to the *Bacillus cereus* sensu lato group. It was described as a novel genomospecies based on whole-genome comparisons and has been isolated from food products, soil, and clinical samples. *B. paranthracis* shares the chromosomal backbone of the *B. cereus* group and may harbour diarrhoeal enterotoxin genes; its distinction from *B. cereus* sensu stricto relies on average nucleotide identity thresholds and is primarily resolved through genomic methods.
