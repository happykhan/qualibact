*Corynebacterium diphtheriae* is a Gram-positive, non-spore-forming, club-shaped rod of the family *Corynebacteriaceae* within the order *Mycobacteriales*, and the causative agent of diphtheria. Toxigenic strains carry the *tox* gene on a lysogenic corynephage, encoding diphtheria toxin which inhibits host protein synthesis and causes the characteristic pseudomembranous pharyngitis and systemic toxicity. *C. diphtheriae* genomes are approximately 2.5 Mb in size and show considerable accessory genome variation, including diverse prophage content and antimicrobial resistance determinants in non-toxigenic invasive lineages.
