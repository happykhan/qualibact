*Streptococcus agalactiae* (group B *Streptococcus*, GBS) is a Gram-positive, beta-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, commonly colonising the gastrointestinal and genitourinary tracts of healthy adults. It is a leading cause of neonatal sepsis, meningitis, and pneumonia, and increasingly causes invasive disease in elderly and immunocompromised adults. Genomically, *S. agalactiae* is classified into ten serotypes based on capsular polysaccharide and displays a well-defined clonal population with a substantial accessory genome encoding pili, surface proteins, and antibiotic resistance determinants.
