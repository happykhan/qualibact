*Streptococcus intermedius* is a Gram-positive coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae* and a member of the *Streptococcus anginosus* group, typically non-haemolytic or alpha-haemolytic. It inhabits the oral cavity and gastrointestinal tract as a commensal, but has a particular tropism for the central nervous system, being the most common anginosus group species isolated from brain abscesses. Genomically, *S. intermedius* produces the human-cell-specific cytolysin intermedilysin and possesses a relatively compact genome with accessory elements linked to its abscess-forming capability.
