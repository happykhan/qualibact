*Streptococcus mutans* is a Gram-positive, facultatively anaerobic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, and the principal aetiological agent of dental caries. It thrives in the oral biofilm by fermenting dietary sugars to lactic acid, producing an acidic microenvironment that demineralises tooth enamel, and synthesises water-insoluble glucans via glucosyltransferases that strengthen plaque adhesion. Genomically, *S. mutans* has a well-characterised genome encoding a suite of acid tolerance, competence, and bacteriocin (mutacin) systems that underpin its competitive fitness in the oral ecosystem.
