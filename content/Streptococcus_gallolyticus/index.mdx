*Streptococcus gallolyticus* is a Gram-positive coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, formerly classified within the *Streptococcus bovis* group, capable of growing in bile-containing media and hydrolysing tannins. It is clinically significant due to its strong association with infective endocarditis and colorectal neoplasia, making its isolation from blood cultures a prompt for colorectal screening. Genomically, *S. gallolyticus* subsp. *gallolyticus* carries pilus operons and collagen-binding proteins that mediate adhesion to damaged heart valves and tumour-associated extracellular matrix.
