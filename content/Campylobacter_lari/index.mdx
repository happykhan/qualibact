*Campylobacter lari* is a Gram-negative, microaerophilic, spiral-shaped rod of the family *Campylobacteraceae*, frequently isolated from seagulls and other wild birds as well as shellfish and surface waters. It is an uncommon but recognised cause of human gastroenteritis and occasional bacteraemia, particularly in immunocompromised individuals. *C. lari* is naturally resistant to nalidixic acid, a feature historically used to distinguish it from *C. jejuni*, and its genome is compact with limited accessory gene content compared to other thermotolerant campylobacters.
