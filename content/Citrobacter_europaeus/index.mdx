*Citrobacter europaeus* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, recently described as a distinct species within the *Citrobacter freundii* complex. It has been isolated from clinical specimens including blood and urine, as well as from environmental sources, and is recognised as an opportunistic pathogen in hospitalised patients. *C. europaeus* carries a chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase typical of the *C. freundii* complex, and genomic analyses have identified it as a phylogenetically coherent lineage distinguishable by whole-genome average nucleotide identity from closely related *Citrobacter* species.
