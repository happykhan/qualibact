*Bacillus velezensis* is an aerobic, endospore-forming Gram-positive rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, closely related to *B. amyloliquefaciens* and *B. subtilis*, with which it was previously confused. It is widely studied as a plant growth-promoting rhizobacterium and biocontrol agent, producing a remarkable diversity of lipopeptides (surfactin, iturin, fengycin), polyketides, and siderophores that suppress phytopathogens. *B. velezensis* devotes up to 13% of its approximately 4.0 Mb genome to secondary metabolite biosynthetic gene clusters, making it one of the richest sources of bioactive compounds among bacilli.
