*Sinorhizobium medicae* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped alphaproteobacterium of the family *Rhizobiaceae*, closely related to *Sinorhizobium meliloti*. It forms nitrogen-fixing root-nodule symbioses predominantly with annual *Medicago* species (medics) and is commonly found in acidic and nutrient-poor soils around the Mediterranean basin. *S. medicae* possesses a multipartite genome consisting of a chromosome and two or more megaplasmids that carry the symbiosis and nitrogen-fixation gene clusters, and shows distinct accessory genome content relative to *S. meliloti*.
