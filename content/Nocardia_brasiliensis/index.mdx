*Nocardia brasiliensis* is a partially acid-fast, Gram-positive filamentous actinobacterium of the family *Nocardiaceae*, commonly isolated from soil in tropical and subtropical regions. It is the leading cause of actinomycetoma, a chronic subcutaneous infection that can extend to bone, particularly in agricultural workers in Latin America. *N. brasiliensis* genomes carry an extensive repertoire of secondary metabolite biosynthetic gene clusters and virulence-associated genes, including those involved in mycolic acid biosynthesis and oxidative stress resistance.
