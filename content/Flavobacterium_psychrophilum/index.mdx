*Flavobacterium psychrophilum* is a Gram-negative, filamentous, non-motile rod in the family *Flavobacteriaceae* within the order *Flavobacteriales*, and an important psychrophilic pathogen of salmonid fish worldwide. It causes bacterial cold-water disease and rainbow trout fry syndrome, leading to significant losses in freshwater aquaculture. *F. psychrophilum* possesses a type IX secretion system and produces extracellular proteases and adhesins, while its relatively small genome (~2.9 Mb) shows limited genetic diversity within clonal complexes despite global distribution.
