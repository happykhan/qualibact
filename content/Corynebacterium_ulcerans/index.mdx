*Corynebacterium ulcerans* is a Gram-positive, non-motile, pleomorphic rod in the family *Corynebacteriaceae*, closely related to *C. diphtheriae* and capable of producing diphtheria toxin via a lysogenic bacteriophage. It is a zoonotic pathogen associated with companion and farm animals, and is increasingly recognised as a cause of diphtheria-like pharyngitis, cutaneous ulcers, and respiratory infections in humans in countries where classical diphtheria has been eliminated. Genomically, *C. ulcerans* strains are diverse, with tox-positive and tox-negative lineages and variable phospholipase D virulence determinants.
