*Enterococcus gallinarum* is a Gram-positive, motile, catalase-negative coccus in the family *Enterococcaceae*, originally isolated from poultry and also found in the intestinal tract of humans. It is an uncommon cause of clinical infections but can produce bacteraemia and endocarditis, particularly in immunocompromised patients. *E. gallinarum* carries the chromosomal *vanC-1* gene conferring intrinsic low-level vancomycin resistance, and its motility is a distinctive phenotypic feature among enterococci.
