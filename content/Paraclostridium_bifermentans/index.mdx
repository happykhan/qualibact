*Paraclostridium bifermentans* (formerly *Clostridium bifermentans*) is a Gram-positive, obligately anaerobic, spore-forming rod of the family *Peptostreptococcaceae*, commonly found in soil, sewage, and the human gut. Although generally considered of low pathogenicity, it has been implicated in wound infections, abscesses, and rare cases of gas gangrene. *P. bifermentans* genomes encode a variety of proteases and fermentation pathways, and certain strains produce mosquitocidal toxins that have attracted interest as potential biological control agents.
