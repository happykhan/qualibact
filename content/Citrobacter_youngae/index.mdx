*Citrobacter youngae* is a facultatively anaerobic, Gram-negative rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, commonly isolated from the intestinal tract of humans and animals as well as from environmental sources. It is an occasional opportunistic pathogen implicated in bloodstream and intra-abdominal infections, particularly in hospitalised patients. *C. youngae* is closely related to *C. freundii* and its genome harbours variable mobile elements, including integrons and plasmids carrying antimicrobial resistance genes.
