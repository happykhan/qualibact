*Mesomycoplasma hyopneumoniae* (formerly *Mycoplasma hyopneumoniae*) is a wall-less, Gram-stain-negative bacterium in the class *Mollicutes*, and the primary causative agent of enzootic pneumonia in pigs. It adheres to ciliated respiratory epithelium, impairing mucociliary clearance and predisposing to secondary bacterial infections, resulting in significant economic losses in swine production. *M. hyopneumoniae* possesses a highly reduced genome of approximately 0.9 Mb with limited metabolic capability, extensive surface-antigen variation, and strain-level diversity in repeat-rich regions.
