*Megasphaera elsdenii* is a large, Gram-negative, strictly anaerobic coccus in the family *Veillonellaceae* (class *Negativicutes*), and one of the principal lactate-utilising bacteria in the rumen and hindgut of cattle and other animals. It plays a key role in preventing ruminal acidosis by fermenting lactate to butyrate, propionate, and other short-chain fatty acids. The genome of *M. elsdenii* (~2.5 Mb) encodes extensive lactate-metabolism pathways and has attracted interest for probiotic and biofuel applications.
