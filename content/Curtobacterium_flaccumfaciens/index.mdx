*Curtobacterium flaccumfaciens* is an aerobic, Gram-positive, non-sporulating, coryneform rod of the family *Microbacteriaceae* within the phylum *Actinomycetota*. The pathovar *flaccumfaciens* is the causative agent of bacterial wilt of dry beans and is a quarantine-listed plant pathogen in many countries, while other strains are common endophytes and epiphytes of various plants. Genomically, *C. flaccumfaciens* possesses a compact genome (~3.5 Mb) with pathogenicity-related plasmids encoding extracellular polysaccharide biosynthesis and plant cell-wall-degrading enzymes in wilt-causing strains.
