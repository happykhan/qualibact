*Lactococcus lactis* is a non-motile, Gram-positive coccus in the family *Streptococcaceae*, best known as the principal starter organism in the production of cheese, buttermilk, and other fermented dairy products. It is generally recognised as safe (GRAS) and is widely used as a model organism for lactic acid bacteria research and as a chassis for recombinant protein expression. The genome of *L. lactis* (~2.3-2.6 Mb) varies considerably between dairy and plant-associated strains, with the dairy lineages showing genome decay driven by adaptation to milk.
