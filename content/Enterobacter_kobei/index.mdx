*Enterobacter kobei* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, classified within the *Enterobacter cloacae* complex. It is an emerging healthcare-associated pathogen recovered from bloodstream infections, respiratory specimens, and wounds, often in patients with underlying comorbidities. *E. kobei* carries an inducible chromosomal AmpC cephalosporinase and has been reported to acquire carbapenem and colistin resistance determinants, posing treatment challenges.
