*Clostridium botulinum* is a Gram-positive, anaerobic, spore-forming rod of the family *Clostridiaceae*, found ubiquitously in soil and aquatic sediments worldwide. It produces botulinum neurotoxin, the most potent biological toxin known, which causes botulism -- a potentially fatal neuroparalytic disease in humans and animals. *C. botulinum* is a genetically heterogeneous species comprising four physiological groups (I-IV) that produce seven major toxin serotypes (A-G), with toxin genes located on chromosomes, plasmids, or bacteriophages.
