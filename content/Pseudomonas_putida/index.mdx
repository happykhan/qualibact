*Pseudomonas putida* is a Gram-negative, aerobic rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, ubiquitous in temperate soil and water habitats and known for its exceptional metabolic versatility. It is a key organism in bioremediation due to its ability to degrade a wide range of aromatic and aliphatic hydrocarbons, and serves as a widely used chassis for industrial and synthetic biology applications. *P. putida* has a large genome (~6 Mb) with extensive catabolic gene networks, multiple stress-response systems, and a generally non-pathogenic profile, though rare opportunistic infections have been documented.
