*Pseudomonas mendocina* is a Gram-negative, aerobic, non-fermenting rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, isolated from soil and water environments. It is a rare opportunistic pathogen, with sporadic reports of endocarditis, bacteraemia, and spondylodiscitis mainly in immunocompromised patients. *P. mendocina* is of biotechnological interest for its toluene-degrading capacity and its use in bioremediation, and possesses a genome encoding diverse catabolic pathways for aromatic compounds.
