*Yersinia pseudotuberculosis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Yersiniaceae* that causes mesenteric lymphadenitis, ileitis, and septicaemia in humans, and is an important pathogen of wild and domestic animals. It is the evolutionary progenitor of *Y. pestis* and shares the pYV virulence plasmid encoding a type III secretion system. *Y. pseudotuberculosis* genomes exhibit greater genetic diversity and a broader host range than *Y. pestis*, with chromosomal pathogenicity islands, superantigenic toxin genes in some lineages, and the capacity for environmental persistence in soil and water.
