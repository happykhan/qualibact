*Streptococcus dysgalactiae* is a Gram-positive, beta-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, with subspecies *equisimilis* (Lancefield groups C and G) being the principal human pathogen. It causes pharyngitis, skin and soft tissue infections, bacteraemia, and, less commonly, streptococcal toxic shock syndrome, with clinical presentations resembling those of *S. pyogenes*. Genomically, *S. dysgalactiae* subsp. *equisimilis* shares extensive virulence gene content with *S. pyogenes* through frequent horizontal gene transfer, including superantigens and M-like proteins.
