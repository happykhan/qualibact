*Photobacterium damselae* is a Gram-negative, halophilic, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Vibrionaceae*, found in marine and estuarine environments worldwide. Its subspecies *P. damselae* subsp. *damselae* causes necrotising fasciitis and fatal septicaemia in humans following wound exposure to seawater, while subsp. *piscicida* is a significant fish pathogen causing pasteurellosis in aquaculture. The genome harbours plasmid-borne virulence determinants including potent haemolysins and phospholipases, with substantial variation in toxin gene content between the two subspecies.
