*Staphylococcus argenteus* is a coagulase-positive, Gram-positive coccus within the family *Staphylococcaceae*, recently recognised as a distinct species closely related to *Staphylococcus aureus*. It is an emerging human pathogen associated with skin and soft tissue infections, bacteraemia, and food-borne outbreaks, and is notable for typically lacking the golden pigment staphyloxanthin. Genomically, *S. argenteus* carries many of the same virulence and resistance determinants as *S. aureus* but displays distinct population structure and phylogeographic lineages.
