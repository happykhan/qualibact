*Fusobacterium nucleatum* is a Gram-negative, obligately anaerobic, spindle-shaped rod in the family *Fusobacteriaceae*, and one of the most abundant anaerobes in the human oral cavity. It plays a central role in dental plaque biofilm formation by co-aggregating with both early and late colonisers, and is implicated in periodontitis, adverse pregnancy outcomes, and, notably, colorectal cancer where it is enriched in tumour tissue. Genomically, *F. nucleatum* exhibits substantial subspecies-level diversity, with the adhesin FadA and the outer-membrane protein Fap2 serving as key virulence determinants.
