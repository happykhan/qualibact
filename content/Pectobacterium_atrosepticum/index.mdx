*Pectobacterium atrosepticum* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Pectobacteriaceae*, primarily known as a phytopathogen of potato in temperate climates. It causes blackleg disease of potato stems and soft rot of tubers through the secretion of plant cell-wall-degrading enzymes, leading to significant agricultural losses. The genome of *P. atrosepticum* encodes multiple type secretion systems and horizontally acquired islands that distinguish it from related soft-rot pathogens and restrict its host range largely to potato.
