*Haemophilus influenzae* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic coccobacillus in the family *Pasteurellaceae*, requiring both X and V growth factors, and a commensal and pathogen of the human respiratory tract. Encapsulated strains, particularly serotype b (Hib), were historically the leading cause of bacterial meningitis in children before widespread vaccination, while non-typeable strains remain a major cause of otitis media, sinusitis, community-acquired pneumonia, and exacerbations of chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. *H. influenzae* has a small, naturally transformable genome (~1.8 Mb) with extensive phase-variable gene regulation and was the first free-living organism to have its genome fully sequenced.
