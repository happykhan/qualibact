*Mycobacterium abscessus* is a rapidly growing, acid-fast bacillus in the family *Mycobacteriaceae*, and the most clinically significant rapidly growing non-tuberculous mycobacterium (NTM). It causes chronic pulmonary infections particularly in individuals with cystic fibrosis or bronchiectasis, as well as skin and soft-tissue infections following trauma or surgery. The genome of *M. abscessus* (~5.1 Mb) is notable for intrinsic resistance to most antibiotics, an inducible *erm*(41) macrolide resistance gene, and a smooth-to-rough colony morphotype switch linked to glycopeptidolipid loss and increased virulence.
