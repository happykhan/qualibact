*Xanthomonas arboricola* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped plant pathogen in the family *Xanthomonadaceae*, causing bacterial spot and blight diseases on a wide range of fruit and nut trees including walnut, stone fruits, and hazelnut. Several pathovars are recognised, with *X. arboricola* pv. *juglandis* and pv. *pruni* being of major agricultural importance. Genomic analyses reveal pathovar-specific type III effector repertoires and variable accessory genome content that underpin host specificity and adaptation to arboreal niches.
