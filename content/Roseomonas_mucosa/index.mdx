*Roseomonas mucosa* is a Gram-negative, pink-pigmented, aerobic coccobacillus of the family *Acetobacteraceae* (order *Rhodospirillales*), found in environmental sources and as a commensal of human skin. It has attracted considerable interest as a potential therapeutic probiotic for atopic dermatitis, with clinical trials demonstrating improvement in skin barrier function and reduction of disease severity upon topical application. *R. mucosa* genomes encode lipid-modifying enzymes and sphingolipid biosynthesis genes thought to mediate its beneficial effects on skin health, alongside a moderate accessory genome reflecting strain-level variation.
