*Pectobacterium brasiliense* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Pectobacteriaceae* (order *Enterobacterales*), and a major plant pathogen causing soft rot and blackleg disease in potato and other crops worldwide. It produces a battery of plant cell-wall-degrading enzymes, including pectinases, cellulases, and proteases, that macerate host tissue. *P. brasiliense* has emerged as one of the most aggressive and geographically widespread species in the genus, with a genome that carries a large complement of type secretion systems and virulence regulators.
