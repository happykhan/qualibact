*Xanthomonas citri* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped plant pathogen of the family *Xanthomonadaceae* and the causal agent of Asiatic citrus canker, a disease of major quarantine significance that causes raised corky lesions on leaves, fruit, and stems of citrus species. It is disseminated by wind-driven rain and contaminated plant material and has been the target of extensive eradication campaigns worldwide. *X. citri* genomes carry a prominent repertoire of type III effectors and transcription activator-like (TAL) effectors, alongside xanthan gum biosynthesis genes and a type IV secretion system involved in virulence.
