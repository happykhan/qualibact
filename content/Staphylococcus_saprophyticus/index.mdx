*Staphylococcus saprophyticus* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, characteristically resistant to novobiocin. It is the second most common cause of uncomplicated urinary tract infections in young, sexually active women, possessing surface-associated adhesins such as UafA and the Ssp lipase that facilitate colonisation of the urogenital epithelium. Genomically, *S. saprophyticus* has a compact genome with a conserved core and species-specific uropathogenicity islands distinct from other coagulase-negative staphylococci.
