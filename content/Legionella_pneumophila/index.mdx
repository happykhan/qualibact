*Legionella pneumophila* is an aerobic, Gram-negative rod in the family *Legionellaceae*, naturally found in freshwater environments where it replicates within free-living amoebae. It is the principal agent of Legionnaires' disease, a severe form of pneumonia acquired by inhaling contaminated aerosols from cooling towers, plumbing systems, and other engineered water sources. The genome of *L. pneumophila* (~3.4 Mb) encodes an exceptionally large Dot/Icm type IV secretion system with over 300 effector proteins that manipulate host cell trafficking.
