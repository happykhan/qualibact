*Peptoniphilus harei* is a Gram-positive, obligately anaerobic coccus of the family *Peptoniphilaceae*, originally described from human clinical specimens. It is an opportunistic pathogen associated with soft-tissue infections, bone and joint infections, and abscesses, often in polymicrobial settings. *P. harei* has a small genome (approximately 1.5--1.8 Mb) with limited biosynthetic capacity, relying on peptone and amino acid fermentation for energy, and is part of the growing group of Gram-positive anaerobic cocci increasingly recognised in clinical microbiology.
