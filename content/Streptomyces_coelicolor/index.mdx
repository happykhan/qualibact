*Streptomyces coelicolor* is a Gram-positive, filamentous, spore-forming bacterium of the family *Streptomycetaceae* within the order *Actinomycetales*, commonly found in soil. It is the genetically best-studied representative of the genus *Streptomyces*, which produces the majority of clinically used natural-product antibiotics, and *S. coelicolor* itself synthesises several bioactive compounds including actinorhodin and undecylprodigiosin. Genomically, it possesses a large, linear chromosome (~8.7 Mb) with a high GC content and over 20 biosynthetic gene clusters for secondary metabolites, making it a model organism for antibiotic discovery and regulation.
