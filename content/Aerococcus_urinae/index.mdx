*Aerococcus urinae* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic coccus in the family *Aerococcaceae*, typically arranged in tetrads or clusters. It is an emerging urinary tract pathogen, most commonly affecting elderly men, and can progress to serious invasive infections including infective endocarditis and bacteraemia if left untreated. *A. urinae* has a relatively small genome and is frequently misidentified as an enterococcus or streptococcus by conventional methods, though MALDI-TOF mass spectrometry and 16S rRNA sequencing have improved its recognition in clinical laboratories.
