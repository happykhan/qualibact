*Streptococcus gordonii* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae* and a member of the mitis group of viridans streptococci. It is a primary coloniser of the oral cavity, playing a key role in dental plaque biofilm formation, but can also cause infective endocarditis when it enters the bloodstream. Genomically, *S. gordonii* possesses a well-characterised genome encoding multiple surface adhesins, competence systems for natural transformation, and cell-wall-anchored proteins that mediate coaggregation with other oral bacteria.
