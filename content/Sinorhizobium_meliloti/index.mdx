*Sinorhizobium meliloti* (also known as *Ensifer meliloti*) is a Gram-negative, aerobic alphaproteobacterium in the family *Rhizobiaceae*, capable of establishing nitrogen-fixing symbiosis with leguminous plants of the genera *Medicago*, *Melilotus*, and *Trigonella*. It is a model organism for studying rhizobium-legume interactions, nodule organogenesis, and biological nitrogen fixation. *S. meliloti* has a multipartite genome comprising a chromosome and two large megaplasmids (pSymA and pSymB) that carry the symbiotic nitrogen-fixation genes and essential metabolic functions, respectively.
