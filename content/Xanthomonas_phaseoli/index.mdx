*Xanthomonas phaseoli* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped bacterium of the family *Xanthomonadaceae* and the causal agent of common bacterial blight of bean (*Phaseolus vulgaris*), one of the most widespread diseases of this crop worldwide. It causes water-soaked leaf lesions and pod spots, resulting in significant yield and seed quality losses. *X. phaseoli* genomes harbour TAL effector genes, type III secretion effectors, and xanthan biosynthesis clusters, with accessory genome variation contributing to pathogenic diversity among strains.
