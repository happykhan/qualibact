*Streptococcus uberis* is a Gram-positive coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, commonly found in the environment of dairy farms including bedding, soil, and bovine skin. It is one of the most prevalent environmental pathogens causing bovine mastitis worldwide, capable of both clinical and subclinical intramammary infections that are often difficult to prevent by conventional teat hygiene alone. Genomically, *S. uberis* displays high strain diversity with a large accessory genome encoding plasminogen-binding proteins, hyaluronic acid capsule, and other virulence factors that facilitate invasion of the mammary gland.
