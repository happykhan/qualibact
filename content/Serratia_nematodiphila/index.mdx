*Serratia nematodiphila* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Yersiniaceae*, originally isolated from the entomopathogenic nematode *Heterorhabditidoides chongmingensis*. It forms a symbiotic association with its nematode host and is toxic to a range of insect larvae, making it of interest for biological pest control. *S. nematodiphila* carries genes encoding insecticidal toxins, chitinases, and secondary metabolites that contribute to its entomopathogenic activity.
