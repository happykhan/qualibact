*Enterococcus faecium* is a Gram-positive, catalase-negative coccus in the family *Enterococcaceae*, found in the gastrointestinal tract of humans and animals. It is a major nosocomial pathogen and a leading cause of vancomycin-resistant enterococcal (VRE) infections, including bacteraemia, endocarditis, and urinary tract infections, and is listed among the WHO priority pathogens for antimicrobial resistance. *E. faecium* is genomically divided into hospital-adapted (clade A1) and community/commensal (clade B) lineages, with hospital strains carrying extensive accessory genomes enriched in resistance and virulence determinants.
