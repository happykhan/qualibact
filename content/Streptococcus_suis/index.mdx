*Streptococcus suis* is a Gram-positive, facultatively anaerobic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, and a major pathogen of pigs causing meningitis, septicaemia, arthritis, and endocarditis. It is also a significant zoonotic agent, particularly in Southeast Asia, where occupational exposure to pigs can lead to severe meningitis and streptococcal toxic shock-like syndrome in humans. Genomically, *S. suis* is classified into at least 29 serotypes based on capsular polysaccharide, with serotype 2 being the most virulent and best studied, and its large pangenome reflects extensive genetic diversity and horizontal gene transfer.
