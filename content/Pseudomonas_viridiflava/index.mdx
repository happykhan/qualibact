*Pseudomonas viridiflava* is a Gram-negative, aerobic rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, widespread as both an epiphyte and an endophyte on a wide range of plant species. It can cause soft rot, leaf spots, and stem necrosis in various crops, including lettuce, tomato, and kiwifruit, and is also commonly found as a saprophyte in soil and water. *P. viridiflava* possesses a type III secretion system and pectolytic enzymes but shows considerable strain-level variation in virulence gene content, reflecting its dual lifestyle as commensal and opportunistic phytopathogen.
