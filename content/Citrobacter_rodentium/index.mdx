*Citrobacter rodentium* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae* that is a natural murine pathogen causing transmissible colonic hyperplasia in mice. It is widely used as a model organism for studying attaching and effacing (A/E) enteric pathogens, including enterohaemorrhagic and enteropathogenic *Escherichia coli*. The genome of *C. rodentium* encodes a locus of enterocyte effacement (LEE) pathogenicity island and a large repertoire of type III secretion system effectors shared with human A/E pathogens.
