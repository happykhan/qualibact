*Acinetobacter nosocomialis* is a non-fermenting, aerobic Gram-negative coccobacillus in the family *Moraxellaceae*, closely related to *A. baumannii* within the *Acinetobacter calcoaceticus*--*baumannii* (ACB) complex. It is an emerging nosocomial pathogen associated with bloodstream infections, pneumonia, and urinary tract infections, particularly in tropical and subtropical regions. *A. nosocomialis* shares many virulence mechanisms with *A. baumannii*, including biofilm formation and natural competence, though it typically exhibits somewhat lower rates of multidrug resistance.
