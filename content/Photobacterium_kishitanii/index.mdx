*Photobacterium kishitanii* is a bioluminescent, Gram-negative rod of the family *Vibrionaceae*, primarily found as a light-organ symbiont and skin-associated bacterium of deep-sea and cold-water fish. It is one of the most widespread luminous symbionts in the ocean, providing light for host counter-illumination camouflage. *P. kishitanii* genomes carry the *lux* operon encoding luciferase and accessory bioluminescence genes, and comparative genomics has revealed adaptations to the low-temperature, high-pressure deep-sea environment including cold-active enzymes and membrane modifications.
