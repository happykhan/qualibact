*Enterococcus avium* is a facultatively anaerobic, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Enterococcaceae*, found in the intestinal tract of birds, mammals, and humans. Although less frequently isolated than *E. faecalis* or *E. faecium*, it is an emerging opportunistic pathogen associated with bacteraemia, endocarditis, and intra-abdominal infections, particularly in immunocompromised patients. Genomically, *E. avium* carries a moderately sized chromosome with mobile genetic elements that can harbour vancomycin and aminoglycoside resistance determinants.
