*Streptococcus vestibularis* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, belonging to the salivarius group of viridans streptococci. It is a commensal of the human oral cavity, primarily colonising the vestibular mucosa, and is only rarely reported as an agent of bacteraemia or infective endocarditis. Genomically, *S. vestibularis* is closely related to *S. salivarius* and *S. thermophilus*, sharing a conserved core genome while possessing species-specific loci that enable its distinction through multilocus sequence analysis.
