*Acinetobacter baumannii* is a strictly aerobic, non-fermenting Gram-negative coccobacillus in the family *Moraxellaceae*, widely recognised as one of the most problematic nosocomial pathogens worldwide. It causes ventilator-associated pneumonia, bloodstream infections, and wound infections, particularly in intensive care settings, and is notorious for its ability to acquire multidrug resistance, including carbapenem resistance. Genomically, *A. baumannii* possesses a highly plastic genome with numerous resistance islands, insertion sequences, and a large accessory genome that facilitates rapid adaptation to hospital environments.
