*Leuconostoc mesenteroides* is a heterofermentative, Gram-positive coccus in the family *Leuconostocaceae*, commonly found on plant surfaces and in fermented foods such as sauerkraut, kimchi, and kefir. It initiates many spontaneous vegetable fermentations due to its early acid production and is industrially important as a producer of dextran.
