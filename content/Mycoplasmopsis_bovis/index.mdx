*Mycoplasmopsis bovis* (formerly *Mycoplasma bovis*) is a wall-less, Gram-stain-negative bacterium in the class *Mollicutes*, and a major pathogen of cattle worldwide. It causes pneumonia, mastitis, arthritis, and otitis media in bovines, leading to substantial economic losses in the dairy and beef industries. *M. bovis* has a small, AT-rich genome (~1 Mb) with extensive variable surface lipoprotein (Vsp) gene families that facilitate immune evasion and contribute to chronic infection.
