*Nocardia cyriacigeorgica* is a partially acid-fast, Gram-positive filamentous actinobacterium of the family *Nocardiaceae*, widely distributed in soil and environmental samples. It is an emerging opportunistic pathogen causing pulmonary, cutaneous, and disseminated nocardiosis, particularly in immunosuppressed individuals. *N. cyriacigeorgica* genomes encode a large number of secondary metabolite biosynthetic gene clusters, lipid metabolism genes, and drug efflux systems, contributing to its characteristic multi-drug resistance profile and environmental adaptability.
