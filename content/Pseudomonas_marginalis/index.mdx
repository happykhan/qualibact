*Pseudomonas marginalis* is a fluorescent, Gram-negative rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, widely distributed in soil and on plant surfaces. It is a significant phytopathogen that causes soft rot in a broad range of vegetables, fruits, and ornamental plants by producing extracellular pectolytic enzymes. *P. marginalis* has a relatively large genome encoding diverse secretion systems and secondary metabolite clusters, contributing to its wide host range and environmental versatility.
