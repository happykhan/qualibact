*Corynebacterium jeikeium* is an aerobic, Gram-positive, non-sporulating, lipophilic rod of the family *Corynebacteriaceae*, commonly found on human skin, particularly in hospitalised and immunocompromised patients. It is a notable opportunistic pathogen responsible for device-related bloodstream infections, endocarditis, and wound infections, with intrinsic resistance to most beta-lactam antibiotics. The genome of *C. jeikeium* is relatively small (~2.5 Mb) and is characterised by a high density of lipid metabolism genes and a paucity of biosynthetic pathways, reflecting its dependence on host-derived lipids.
