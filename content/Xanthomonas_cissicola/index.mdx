*Xanthomonas cissicola* is a Gram-negative, aerobic rod of the family *Xanthomonadaceae*, originally described as the causal agent of bacterial leaf spot on *Cissus* species (grape ivy). It produces the yellow xanthomonadin pigments characteristic of the genus and has been reported to infect ornamental and tropical plants. *X. cissicola* has a moderately sized genome with type III secretion system components and effector genes typical of phytopathogenic xanthomonads.
