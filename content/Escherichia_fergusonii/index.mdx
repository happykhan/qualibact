*Escherichia fergusonii* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, and the closest known phylogenetic relative of *Escherichia coli*. It is found in the intestinal tract of animals and in environmental sources, and has been associated with opportunistic infections in humans including bacteraemia, urinary tract infections, and wound infections. *E. fergusonii* can carry virulence and antimicrobial resistance genes similar to those found in pathogenic *E. coli*, and is likely under-reported due to phenotypic misidentification.
