*Neisseria meningitidis* is an aerobic, encapsulated, Gram-negative diplococcus in the family *Neisseriaceae*, carried asymptomatically in the nasopharynx of approximately 10% of the population. It is a leading cause of bacterial meningitis and fulminant septicaemia (meningococcaemia), with epidemics particularly affecting the African meningitis belt and military recruits. The genome of *N. meningitidis* (~2.3 Mb) is highly recombinogenic and naturally transformable, with 13 capsular serogroups defined by polysaccharide biosynthesis loci, of which serogroups A, B, C, W, X, and Y account for nearly all invasive disease.
