*Enterobacter cloacae* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, widely distributed in soil, water, sewage, and the human gastrointestinal tract. It is a clinically important opportunistic pathogen and a leading cause of nosocomial bacteraemia, pneumonia, urinary tract infections, and surgical-site infections, particularly in intensive care settings. *E. cloacae* is the type species of the *E. cloacae* complex and carries an inducible chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase, with frequent acquisition of carbapenemases and extended-spectrum beta-lactamases on mobile elements.
