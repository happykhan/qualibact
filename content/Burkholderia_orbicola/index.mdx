*Burkholderia orbicola* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting rod recently reclassified within the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc), previously known as *Burkholderia* genomovar or related Bcc taxa. It has been isolated from both environmental sources and clinical specimens, including respiratory samples from cystic fibrosis patients. As with other Bcc species, *B. orbicola* carries a large, multi-replicon genome with intrinsic multidrug resistance determinants and substantial metabolic versatility.
