*Staphylococcus pseudintermedius* is a coagulase-positive, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, and the primary commensal staphylococcal species of dogs. It is a leading cause of canine pyoderma, otitis externa, and surgical site infections, with methicillin-resistant strains (MRSP) increasingly prevalent and posing a One Health concern due to occasional zoonotic transmission. Genomically, *S. pseudintermedius* carries a diverse set of virulence factors including exfoliative toxins and leukocidins, and exhibits a clonal population structure with globally disseminated multidrug-resistant lineages.
