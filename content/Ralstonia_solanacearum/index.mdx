*Ralstonia solanacearum* is a Gram-negative, aerobic rod in the order *Burkholderiales*, and one of the world's most destructive plant-pathogenic bacteria, causing bacterial wilt in over 200 plant species including potato, tomato, and banana. It invades the plant vascular system through roots, producing copious exopolysaccharides that block xylem vessels and lead to wilting. The *R. solanacearum* species complex has been reclassified into three distinct species, and its bipartite genome shows extensive type III effector diversity and horizontal gene transfer that underpin its remarkably broad host range.
