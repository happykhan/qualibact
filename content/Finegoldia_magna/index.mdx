*Finegoldia magna* is a Gram-positive, obligately anaerobic coccus and the sole species in the genus *Finegoldia*, belonging to the family *Peptoniphilaceae*. It is a commensal of human skin and mucosal surfaces but is also a significant opportunistic pathogen, frequently isolated from soft-tissue infections, surgical-site infections, prosthetic joint infections, and abscesses. *F. magna* possesses virulence factors including a subtilisin-like protease (SufA) and protein L, which binds immunoglobulin light chains to evade host immunity.
