*Chlamydia psittaci* is a Gram-negative, obligate intracellular bacterium of the family *Chlamydiaceae* and the causative agent of psittacosis (ornithosis), a zoonotic respiratory disease acquired primarily through inhalation of aerosolised secretions from infected birds. It has a broad avian host range and can cause severe atypical pneumonia in humans, occasionally complicated by endocarditis or encephalitis. *C. psittaci* has a reduced genome of approximately 1.2 Mb and displays considerable genetic diversity across its recognised genotypes (A-F, E/B, M56, WC), which broadly correspond to avian host preference.
