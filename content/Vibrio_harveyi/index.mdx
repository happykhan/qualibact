*Vibrio harveyi* is a bioluminescent, Gram-negative curved rod of the family *Vibrionaceae*, widely distributed in tropical and subtropical marine waters. It is a major pathogen of farmed shrimp, fish, and other marine invertebrates, causing luminous vibriosis with high mortality, and has served as a model organism for the study of quorum sensing. *V. harveyi* genomes encode multiple quorum-sensing circuits, type III and type VI secretion systems, and a suite of haemolysins and proteases that contribute to its virulence in aquaculture settings.
