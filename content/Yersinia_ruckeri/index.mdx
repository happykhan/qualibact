*Yersinia ruckeri* is a Gram-negative rod of the family *Yersiniaceae* and the aetiological agent of enteric redmouth disease (ERM) in salmonid fish, causing significant losses in freshwater aquaculture worldwide. It persists in aquatic environments and can be carried asymptomatically by recovered fish, complicating eradication. *Y. ruckeri* genomes encode virulence factors including an anti-feeding metalloprotease (Yrp1), iron-acquisition systems, and a type III secretion system, with biotype variation linked to motility loss and vaccine escape.
