*Yersinia intermedia* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Yersiniaceae*, commonly found in freshwater, soil, and food sources. It is generally considered non-pathogenic or of low virulence in humans, but has occasionally been isolated from clinical specimens including stool, wounds, and blood, particularly in immunocompromised individuals. *Y. intermedia* lacks the pYV virulence plasmid and major pathogenicity islands found in *Yersinia enterocolitica* and *Yersinia pseudotuberculosis*, and its genome reflects an environmental rather than a host-adapted lifestyle.
