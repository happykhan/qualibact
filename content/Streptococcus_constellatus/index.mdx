*Streptococcus constellatus* is a Gram-positive coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae* and a member of the *Streptococcus anginosus* group, displaying variable haemolysis. It inhabits the oral cavity, upper respiratory tract, and gastrointestinal tract as a commensal, but is clinically significant as a cause of abscesses, empyema, and other pyogenic infections. Genomically, *S. constellatus* shares a conserved core genome with other anginosus group members while carrying species-specific accessory genes related to tissue invasion and purulence.
