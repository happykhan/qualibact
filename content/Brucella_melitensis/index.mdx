*Brucella melitensis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively intracellular coccobacillus of the family *Brucellaceae* within the order *Hyphomicrobiales*. It is the most virulent *Brucella* species in humans, primarily acquired through contact with infected sheep and goats or consumption of unpasteurised dairy products, causing undulant fever (brucellosis). *B. melitensis* has a bipartite genome consisting of two circular chromosomes with remarkably high sequence conservation across strains, reflecting its clonal population structure and intracellular lifestyle.
