*Klebsiella pneumoniae* is a Gram-negative, encapsulated, non-motile rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, and one of the most important opportunistic pathogens worldwide. It causes a wide range of infections including pneumonia, urinary tract infections, bloodstream infections, liver abscesses, and neonatal sepsis, and hypervirulent strains can cause severe community-acquired disease in otherwise healthy individuals. *K. pneumoniae* is a critical priority pathogen for antimicrobial resistance, with the global spread of carbapenemase-producing (KPC, NDM, OXA-48-like) and hypervirulent lineages driven by extensive plasmid-mediated horizontal gene transfer and diverse capsular polysaccharide serotypes.
