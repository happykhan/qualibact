*Agrobacterium tumefaciens* is an aerobic Gram-negative rod in the family *Rhizobiaceae*, widely found in soil and the rhizosphere. It is the causative agent of crown gall disease in dicotyledonous plants, transferring its T-DNA from the tumour-inducing (Ti) plasmid into host plant cells -- a mechanism that has been extensively exploited for plant genetic engineering. *A. tumefaciens* has a bipartite genome consisting of a circular and a linear chromosome plus the Ti plasmid, and its type IV secretion system for DNA transfer is among the best-characterised bacterial conjugation machineries.
