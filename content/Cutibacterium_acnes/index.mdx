*Cutibacterium acnes* (formerly *Propionibacterium acnes*) is a Gram-positive, anaerobic to aerotolerant, non-spore-forming rod in the family *Propionibacteriaceae*, and one of the most abundant members of the human skin microbiota, particularly in sebaceous follicles. It is best known for its role in acne vulgaris but is also an increasingly recognised cause of prosthetic joint infections, endocarditis, and post-surgical implant infections. *C. acnes* exhibits phylogenetically distinct lineages (types IA, IB, II, III) with differing associations to health and disease, and harbours virulence factors including Christie-Atkins-Munch-Petersen (CAMP) factors and lipases.
