*Klebsiella oxytoca* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic, encapsulated rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, found in the environment, food, and the human gastrointestinal tract. It is the second most common *Klebsiella* species in clinical settings, causing urinary tract infections, bacteraemia, and notably antibiotic-associated haemorrhagic colitis mediated by the cytotoxin tilivalline. *K. oxytoca* is the type species of a broader species complex that includes *K. michiganensis* and *K. grimontii*, and possesses a chromosomal class A beta-lactamase (OXY) that confers intrinsic resistance to amino- and carboxy-penicillins.
