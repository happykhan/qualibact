*Aliivibrio fischeri* (formerly *Vibrio fischeri*) is a facultatively anaerobic, bioluminescent Gram-negative rod in the family *Vibrionaceae*, found as a free-living organism in temperate and subtropical marine waters. It is best known for its mutualistic symbiosis with the Hawaiian bobtail squid *Euprymna scolopes*, where it colonises the light organ and produces bioluminescence via the *lux* operon regulated by acyl-homoserine lactone quorum sensing. *A. fischeri* has served as a foundational model for studying quorum sensing, symbiosis, and bioluminescence, and its genome includes two chromosomes typical of the *Vibrionaceae*.
