*Janthinobacterium lividum* is an aerobic, Gram-negative rod of the family *Oxalobacteraceae* within the order *Burkholderiales*, commonly found in soil, freshwater, and glacial environments. It is best known for producing violacein, a purple bisindole pigment with potent antifungal, antibacterial, and antiprotozoal properties, which has attracted considerable interest for biocontrol and pharmaceutical applications. The genome of *J. lividum* is approximately 6 Mb and encodes the five-gene *vioABCDE* operon responsible for violacein biosynthesis, along with diverse secondary metabolite clusters.
