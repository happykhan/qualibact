*Moraxella bovoculi* is a Gram-negative, aerobic, non-motile coccobacillus in the family *Moraxellaceae*, isolated from the conjunctiva and nasal passages of cattle. It is an important veterinary pathogen, frequently associated with infectious bovine keratoconjunctivitis (pinkeye), either as a primary agent or as a co-pathogen with *Moraxella bovis*. The genome of *M. bovoculi* carries genes encoding RTX-family cytotoxins, pilus assembly systems, and mobile genetic elements that contribute to virulence and strain diversity.
