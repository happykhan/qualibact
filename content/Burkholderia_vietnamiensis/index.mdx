*Burkholderia vietnamiensis* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting rod of the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc), first isolated from the rhizosphere of rice in Vietnam. It is notable for its nitrogen-fixing ability and plant growth-promoting properties, making it of agricultural interest, though it can also act as an opportunistic pathogen in cystic fibrosis patients. *B. vietnamiensis* carries a large multi-replicon genome encoding both symbiotic nitrogen fixation (*nif*) genes and typical Bcc virulence and resistance determinants.
