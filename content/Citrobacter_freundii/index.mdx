*Citrobacter freundii* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, widely distributed in soil, water, sewage, and the human gastrointestinal tract. It is a clinically significant opportunistic pathogen causing urinary tract infections, pneumonia, bacteraemia, and neonatal meningitis, with increasing importance in healthcare-associated infections. *C. freundii* carries a chromosomally encoded inducible AmpC beta-lactamase and frequently acquires plasmid-mediated extended-spectrum beta-lactamases and carbapenemases, making it a growing antimicrobial resistance concern.
