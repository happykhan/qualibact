*Aliarcobacter butzleri* (formerly *Arcobacter butzleri*) is a microaerophilic, Gram-negative spiral or curved rod in the family *Arcobacteraceae* within the order *Campylobacterales*. It is an emerging foodborne and waterborne pathogen associated with gastroenteritis, bacteraemia, and sporadic cases of peritonitis in humans, and has been isolated from poultry, livestock, and environmental water sources. *A. butzleri* has a relatively compact genome of approximately 2.3 Mb, with virulence-related genes homologous to those of *Campylobacter jejuni* and considerable genomic diversity among strains.
