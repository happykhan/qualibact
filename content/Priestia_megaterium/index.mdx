*Priestia megaterium* (formerly *Bacillus megaterium*) is a Gram-positive, aerobic, endospore-forming rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, and one of the largest known bacteria, with cells reaching up to 4 micrometres in length. It is widely distributed in soil and has been used extensively in industrial biotechnology for recombinant protein production, vitamin B12 biosynthesis, and as a biocontrol agent. *P. megaterium* possesses a large genome (~5.1 Mb) with diverse secondary metabolite clusters and a well-characterised xylose-inducible expression system.
