*Staphylococcus equorum* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus in the family *Staphylococcaceae*, originally isolated from the skin of horses. It is frequently found in fermented foods such as smear-ripened cheeses, cured sausages, and fermented fish, where it contributes to flavour development and is generally regarded as non-pathogenic. *S. equorum* possesses a genome of approximately 2.7 Mb and is notable for salt tolerance, lipase and protease production, and the absence of major virulence factors typical of pathogenic staphylococci.
