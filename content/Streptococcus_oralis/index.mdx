*Streptococcus oralis* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae* within the mitis group of viridans streptococci. It is a commensal of the human oral cavity and an early coloniser of dental surfaces, but can cause infective endocarditis and is an increasingly reported agent of penicillin-resistant bacteraemia in immunocompromised patients. Genomically, *S. oralis* is naturally transformable with a highly recombinogenic genome, and recent taxonomic revisions have subdivided it into subspecies including *subsp. tigurinus* and *subsp. dentisani*.
