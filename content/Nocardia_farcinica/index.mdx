*Nocardia farcinica* is a partially acid-fast, Gram-positive filamentous actinobacterium of the family *Nocardiaceae*, found in soil and the causative agent of nocardiosis in both humans and animals. It is one of the most virulent *Nocardia* species, frequently causing disseminated infections involving the lungs, brain, and skin, especially in immunocompromised hosts. *N. farcinica* was the first *Nocardia* species to have its genome fully sequenced, revealing a large chromosome (approximately 6.3 Mb) with extensive gene duplication, numerous drug resistance determinants, and a rich set of secondary metabolite gene clusters.
