*Enterococcus mundtii* is a Gram-positive, catalase-negative coccus in the family *Enterococcaceae*, typically producing a yellow pigment that distinguishes it from most other enterococci. It is commonly isolated from plant material, soil, and the intestinal tract of insects and animals, and is rarely associated with human infection. *E. mundtii* is noted for producing the broad-spectrum bacteriocin mundticin, which has attracted interest for potential biopreservation applications.
