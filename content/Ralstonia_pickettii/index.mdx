*Ralstonia pickettii* is a Gram-negative, aerobic, non-fermenting rod in the order *Burkholderiales*, commonly found in water, soil, and clinical water systems including purified and distilled water supplies. It is a low-virulence opportunistic pathogen responsible for outbreaks linked to contaminated pharmaceutical solutions, intravenous fluids, and hospital water lines, causing bacteraemia and respiratory infections in immunocompromised patients. *R. pickettii* tolerates oligotrophic conditions and its genome reflects adaptation to nutrient-poor aqueous environments, with limited accessory virulence content compared to the related plant pathogen *R. solanacearum*.
