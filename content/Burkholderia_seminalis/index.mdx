*Burkholderia seminalis* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting rod of the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc), initially isolated from rice seeds and clinical specimens. It occupies a dual ecological niche as both a plant-associated bacterium with antifungal properties and an opportunistic pathogen in immunocompromised individuals, particularly those with cystic fibrosis. The genome of *B. seminalis* is organised across multiple replicons and encodes secondary metabolite clusters, including those for antifungal compounds, alongside typical Bcc virulence factors.
