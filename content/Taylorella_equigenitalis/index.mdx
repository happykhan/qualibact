*Taylorella equigenitalis* is a Gram-negative, microaerophilic coccobacillus of the family *Alcaligenaceae* within the order *Burkholderiales*. It is the causative agent of contagious equine metritis (CEM), a notifiable sexually transmitted disease of horses that causes mucopurulent vaginal discharge and temporary infertility in mares. Genomically, *T. equigenitalis* has a relatively small genome (~1.6 Mb) with limited metabolic versatility, reflecting its obligate association with the equine reproductive tract, and shows low genetic diversity across global isolates.
