*Bacillus wiedmannii* is a facultatively anaerobic, endospore-forming Gram-positive rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, a member of the *Bacillus cereus* sensu lato group first described from dairy products and food processing environments. It has been associated with food spoilage and may carry enterotoxin genes (Hbl, Nhe) similar to those of *B. cereus*, raising potential food safety concerns. *B. wiedmannii* is delineated from other *B. cereus* group members primarily through whole-genome average nucleotide identity analysis, and its ecological role in soil and food matrices is still being defined.
