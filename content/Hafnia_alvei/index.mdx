*Hafnia alvei* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Hafniaceae* within the order *Enterobacterales*, widely distributed in the environment, foods (especially dairy and meat products), and the gastrointestinal tract of humans and animals. It is an uncommon opportunistic pathogen but can cause bacteraemia, respiratory tract infections, and wound infections, particularly in debilitated or immunocompromised hosts. *H. alvei* possesses a chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase and has been reported to acquire extended-spectrum resistance determinants on mobile genetic elements.
