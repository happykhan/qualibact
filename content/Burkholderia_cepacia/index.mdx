*Burkholderia cepacia* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting rod of the family *Burkholderiaceae*, originally described as a phytopathogen causing soft rot of onions. It is the namesake of the *B. cepacia* complex (Bcc), a group of closely related species that are opportunistic pathogens in immunocompromised individuals and people with cystic fibrosis. *B. cepacia* possesses a large, multi-replicon genome with broad metabolic versatility and intrinsic resistance to many antibiotics, including polymyxins.
