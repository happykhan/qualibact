*Streptococcus salivarius* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae* within the salivarius group of viridans streptococci. It is one of the earliest and most abundant colonisers of the human oral mucosa and tongue, generally considered a commensal with probiotic potential, though it can occasionally cause bacteraemia and endocarditis. Genomically, *S. salivarius* possesses genes encoding urease, fructosyltransferase, and bacteriocins (salivaricins) that contribute to its ecological fitness in the oral and upper respiratory niches.
