*Prevotella intermedia* is a Gram-negative, obligately anaerobic, pigmented rod in the family *Prevotellaceae*, commonly inhabiting the oral cavity and upper respiratory tract. It is strongly associated with periodontal disease, acute necrotising ulcerative gingivitis, and various orofacial infections, where it participates in polymicrobial biofilms. *P. intermedia* possesses virulence factors including interpain A (a cysteine protease), lipopolysaccharide, and haemagglutinins, and its genome shows evidence of considerable horizontal gene transfer with other oral anaerobes.
