*Vibrio parahaemolyticus* is a halophilic, Gram-negative curved rod within the family *Vibrionaceae* and a leading cause of seafood-associated gastroenteritis worldwide. It is naturally present in warm coastal waters and becomes clinically significant through consumption of raw or undercooked shellfish, particularly oysters. Pathogenic strains typically carry thermostable direct haemolysin (TDH) and TDH-related haemolysin (TRH) genes on type III secretion system islands, and *V. parahaemolyticus* genomes show substantial serotype diversity driven by recombination of O- and K-antigen loci.
