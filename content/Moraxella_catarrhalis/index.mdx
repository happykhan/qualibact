*Moraxella catarrhalis* is an aerobic, Gram-negative diplococcus in the family *Moraxellaceae* (order *Pseudomonadales*), and a common commensal of the human upper respiratory tract. It is the third most frequent bacterial cause of acute otitis media in children and is an important trigger of exacerbations in chronic obstructive pulmonary disease. The genome of *M. catarrhalis* (~1.9 Mb) reveals two major phylogenetic clades with differing virulence factor profiles, and nearly all clinical isolates produce chromosomally encoded beta-lactamases.
