*Listeria seeligeri* is a Gram-positive, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Listeriaceae*, found in soil, vegetation, and water, and generally considered non-pathogenic or very rarely pathogenic for humans. Although it harbours a truncated version of the LIPI-1 virulence cluster including a listeriolysin O gene, it lacks key internalin genes and is unable to replicate intracellularly under normal conditions. The genome of *L. seeligeri* (~2.8 Mb) provides a valuable evolutionary comparison for understanding how virulence was acquired and lost across the *Listeria* genus.
