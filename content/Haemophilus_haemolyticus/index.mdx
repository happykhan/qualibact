*Haemophilus haemolyticus* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic coccobacillus in the family *Pasteurellaceae*, and a commensal of the human upper respiratory tract that requires both X (haemin) and V (NAD) growth factors. It is generally non-pathogenic but is frequently misidentified as non-typeable *Haemophilus influenzae* by routine laboratory methods, complicating surveillance and epidemiological studies. Genomic comparisons have revealed that *H. haemolyticus* can be reliably differentiated from *H. influenzae* through the absence of certain virulence-associated loci and by whole-genome average nucleotide identity.
