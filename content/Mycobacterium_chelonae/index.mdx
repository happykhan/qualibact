*Mycobacterium chelonae* is a rapidly growing, acid-fast bacillus in the family *Mycobacteriaceae*, widely distributed in water, soil, and biofilms, including municipal water systems. It is an opportunistic pathogen that most commonly causes skin and soft-tissue infections following surgery, cosmetic procedures, or catheter use, and disseminated disease in immunosuppressed patients. The genome of *M. chelonae* (~5.0 Mb) carries intrinsic resistance to several antibiotics and shares close phylogenetic affinity with *M. abscessus*, though the two species differ in their *erm* gene functionality and clinical spectrum.
