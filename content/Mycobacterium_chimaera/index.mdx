*Mycobacterium chimaera* is a slow-growing, acid-fast bacillus in the family *Mycobacteriaceae*, belonging to the *Mycobacterium avium* complex (MAC). It gained global attention as the cause of invasive infections linked to contaminated heater-cooler units used during cardiac surgery, with cases of prosthetic valve endocarditis and disseminated disease reported worldwide. The genome of *M. chimaera* (~5.6 Mb) is closely related to *M. intracellulare* but is distinguished by specific genomic regions, and outbreak investigations have revealed remarkably low genetic diversity among heater-cooler-associated isolates, pointing to a single-source contamination event.
