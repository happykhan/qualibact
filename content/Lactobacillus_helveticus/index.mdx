*Lactobacillus helveticus* is a thermophilic, homofermentative Gram-positive rod in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, extensively used in the manufacture of Swiss-type and Italian hard cheeses. It is distinguished by a highly active cell-envelope proteinase system that releases bioactive peptides during milk fermentation. The genome of *L. helveticus* (~2.1 Mb) is characterised by numerous IS elements, extensive gene decay, and a pronounced adaptation to the dairy niche.
