*Streptococcus sanguinis* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, belonging to the mitis group of viridans streptococci. It is a pioneer coloniser of the tooth surface and dental plaque, and is the viridans streptococcal species most frequently associated with infective endocarditis following transient bacteraemia. Genomically, *S. sanguinis* encodes surface proteins including SsaB and fimbriae-associated adhesins that mediate binding to salivary pellicle and platelet aggregation, and it is naturally competent for genetic transformation.
