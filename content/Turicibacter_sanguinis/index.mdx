*Turicibacter sanguinis* is a Gram-positive, obligately anaerobic, non-motile rod in the family *Erysipelotrichaceae* (phylum *Bacillota*), first isolated from a blood culture of a febrile patient. It is increasingly recognised as a common member of the mammalian gut microbiota, where it interacts with host bile acids and serotonin signalling pathways, influencing lipid metabolism and immune homeostasis. *T. sanguinis* has a relatively small genome (approximately 2.9 Mb) and is difficult to cultivate, which has limited its characterisation until recent culture-independent and gnotobiotic studies.
