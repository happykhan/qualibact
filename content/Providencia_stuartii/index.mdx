*Providencia stuartii* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Morganellaceae*, commonly found in the gastrointestinal tract and frequently associated with healthcare settings. It is a notable cause of catheter-associated urinary tract infections and nosocomial outbreaks, particularly among long-term care residents, and exhibits intrinsic resistance to multiple antibiotic classes including colistin and some aminoglycosides. The genome of *P. stuartii* readily acquires mobile resistance elements, making it an increasingly difficult-to-treat nosocomial pathogen.
