*Lactiplantibacillus plantarum* is a Gram-positive, facultatively heterofermentative rod belonging to the family *Lactobacillaceae*, ubiquitous in fermented foods, plant material, and the mammalian gastrointestinal tract. It is one of the most versatile lactic acid bacteria, widely used in food fermentation, biopreservation, and as a probiotic. *L. plantarum* possesses one of the largest genomes among lactobacilli, with an exceptionally open pangenome and extensive lifestyle-adaptation genes.
