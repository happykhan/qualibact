*Pediococcus acidilactici* is a Gram-positive, homofermentative coccus in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, commonly found in fermented foods such as sausages, cheese, and silage. It is used as a starter culture and probiotic and is notable for producing pediocin PA-1, a class IIa bacteriocin with potent anti-listerial activity. The genome of *P. acidilactici* is relatively small (~2 Mb) with strain-level variation in bacteriocin gene clusters and carbohydrate utilisation operons.
