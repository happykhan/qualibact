*Streptococcus pyogenes* (group A *Streptococcus*, GAS) is a Gram-positive, beta-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, classified as Lancefield group A. It causes a broad range of human diseases from pharyngitis and impetigo to life-threatening necrotising fasciitis, streptococcal toxic shock syndrome, and the post-infectious sequelae acute rheumatic fever and glomerulonephritis. Genomically, *S. pyogenes* is typed by the hypervariable *emm* gene encoding M protein, has a relatively conserved core genome, and carries numerous prophage-encoded superantigens and virulence factors that drive strain-specific pathogenicity.
