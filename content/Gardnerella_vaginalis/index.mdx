*Gardnerella vaginalis* is a Gram-variable, facultatively anaerobic, short rod in the family *Bifidobacteriaceae* within the order *Bifidobacteriales*, and the principal organism associated with bacterial vaginosis. It produces a cytotoxin called vaginolysin and forms tenacious biofilms on vaginal epithelial cells, contributing to the characteristic clue cells seen on microscopy. Recent genomic analyses have revealed that *G. vaginalis* comprises multiple genomic clades with differing virulence potential, supporting its reclassification into several distinct species.
