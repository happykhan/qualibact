*Brucella suis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively intracellular coccobacillus of the family *Brucellaceae*, primarily causing brucellosis in swine but also infecting cattle, hares, reindeer, and wild boar depending on the biovar. In humans, infection occurs through occupational exposure or consumption of contaminated animal products and can cause a debilitating systemic illness. *B. suis* has a bipartite genome of approximately 3.3 Mb and is the most genetically diverse classical *Brucella* species, with five biovars that show host preference differences and are distinguishable by genomic and phenotypic markers.
