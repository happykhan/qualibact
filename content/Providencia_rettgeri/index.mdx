*Providencia rettgeri* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Morganellaceae*, found in water, soil, and the intestinal flora of humans and animals. It is an emerging multidrug-resistant opportunistic pathogen that causes urinary tract infections, wound infections, and bacteraemia, predominantly in hospitalised and immunocompromised patients. *P. rettgeri* is of particular concern for harbouring plasmid-mediated carbapenemase genes such as NDM-1, contributing to the global dissemination of carbapenem resistance among Enterobacterales.
