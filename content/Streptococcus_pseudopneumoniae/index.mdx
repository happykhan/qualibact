*Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic coccus in the family *Streptococcaceae*, phenotypically intermediate between *Streptococcus pneumoniae* and *Streptococcus mitis*. It colonises the human upper respiratory tract and has been recovered from sputum of patients with chronic obstructive pulmonary disease, lower respiratory tract infections, and occasionally blood cultures. *S. pseudopneumoniae* is optochin-sensitive in CO2 but resistant in ambient air, bile-insoluble, and lacks a true pneumococcal capsule, and its genome shows extensive recombination with both *S. pneumoniae* and *S. mitis*.
