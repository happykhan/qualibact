*Nocardia nova* is a partially acid-fast, Gram-positive, filamentous actinomycete in the family *Nocardiaceae* (order *Corynebacteriales*), found in soil and aquatic environments worldwide. It is an opportunistic pathogen that primarily causes pulmonary nocardiosis in immunocompromised patients, with potential for dissemination to the brain and skin. The genome of *N. nova* (~6.5 Mb) is GC-rich and encodes a diverse secondary metabolite repertoire; among clinically relevant *Nocardia* species, *N. nova* is notable for its relatively predictable susceptibility to trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole, the first-line treatment for nocardiosis.
