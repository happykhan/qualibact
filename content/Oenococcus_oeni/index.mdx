*Oenococcus oeni* is a Gram-positive, facultatively anaerobic, lactic acid coccus in the family *Lactobacillaceae* (formerly *Leuconostocaceae*), uniquely adapted to the harsh physicochemical conditions of wine. It is the principal agent of malolactic fermentation, converting L-malic acid to L-lactic acid and thereby modulating wine acidity, flavour, and microbial stability. *O. oeni* has a small genome (~1.8 Mb) with significant strain-level variation in genes involved in stress tolerance, sugar utilisation, and flavour compound production.
