*Lactobacillus gasseri* is a Gram-positive, homofermentative rod in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, commonly found in the human gastrointestinal and urogenital tracts. It is considered a beneficial commensal with probiotic potential, contributing to pathogen exclusion and immune homeostasis. *L. gasseri* has a moderately sized genome encoding bacteriocin biosynthesis clusters and CRISPR-Cas defence systems that vary among strains.
