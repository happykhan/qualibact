*Aeromonas caviae* is a facultatively anaerobic, oxidase-positive Gram-negative rod in the family *Aeromonadaceae*, commonly found in freshwater, sewage, and food products. It is one of the most frequently isolated *Aeromonas* species from human gastrointestinal infections, particularly causing self-limiting diarrhoea in children, and can occasionally produce wound infections and bacteraemia. *A. caviae* typically carries a chromosomal metallo-beta-lactamase gene and possesses a diverse set of virulence factors, though it generally lacks the aerolysin toxin found in other pathogenic aeromonads.
