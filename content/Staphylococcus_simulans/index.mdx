*Staphylococcus simulans* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, found on the skin of humans and various animals. It is an opportunistic pathogen associated with prosthetic joint infections, endocarditis, and bovine mastitis, and is capable of producing the antimicrobial peptide lysostaphin. Genomically, *S. simulans* carries a moderately sized genome with mobile genetic elements that can harbour methicillin-resistance determinants, and its lysostaphin gene cluster has attracted interest for potential anti-staphylococcal applications.
