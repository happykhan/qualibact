*Lactobacillus rhamnosus* is a facultatively heterofermentative, Gram-positive rod in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, found in the human gastrointestinal tract and various dairy products. The strain *L. rhamnosus* GG is among the most studied and commercially used probiotics worldwide, recognised for its adhesion to intestinal mucus and immunomodulatory effects. Its genome (~3.0 Mb) is relatively large for the genus and features a flexible accessory genome with numerous genes for carbohydrate utilisation and pili-mediated host interaction.
