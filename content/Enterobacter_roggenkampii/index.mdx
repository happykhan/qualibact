*Enterobacter roggenkampii* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, a more recently delineated member of the *Enterobacter cloacae* complex. It is increasingly recognised as a cause of nosocomial bloodstream, urinary tract, and respiratory infections, and is often misidentified as *E. cloacae* by conventional phenotypic methods. *E. roggenkampii* possesses a chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase and has been associated with outbreaks involving carbapenemase-producing strains in hospital settings.
