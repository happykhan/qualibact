*Bacteroides uniformis* is an obligately anaerobic, non-spore-forming Gram-negative rod in the family *Bacteroidaceae*, and a prevalent member of the human colonic microbiota. It is involved in the fermentation of dietary fibre and resistant starch, and has been associated in clinical and metagenomic studies with metabolic health benefits, including improved glucose homeostasis and reduced adiposity. *B. uniformis* encodes a diverse set of polysaccharide utilisation loci and has been investigated as a potential next-generation probiotic, with its genome reflecting adaptation to the competitive glycan-rich environment of the human gut.
