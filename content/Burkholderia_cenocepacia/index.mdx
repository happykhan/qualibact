*Burkholderia cenocepacia* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting, motile rod of the family *Burkholderiaceae*, found in soil, water, and the rhizosphere. It is a prominent member of the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc) and an important opportunistic pathogen in individuals with cystic fibrosis, where it can cause severe necrotising pneumonia and the fatal "cepacia syndrome." *B. cenocepacia* has a large, multi-replicon genome (typically three chromosomes plus plasmids) with extensive genomic plasticity, numerous genomic islands, and intrinsic multidrug resistance determinants.
