*Brucella ovis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively intracellular coccobacillus of the family *Brucellaceae*, causing epididymitis and infertility in rams and occasional abortion in ewes. Unlike most other *Brucella* species, it is not considered zoonotic and does not typically infect humans. *B. ovis* has a bipartite genome with a naturally rough lipopolysaccharide phenotype due to the absence of a functional O-antigen, and shows evidence of further genome reduction compared to smooth *Brucella* species through accumulation of pseudogenes and IS element insertions.
