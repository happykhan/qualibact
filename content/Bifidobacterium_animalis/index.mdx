*Bifidobacterium animalis* is an anaerobic, Gram-positive, non-motile rod belonging to the family *Bifidobacteriaceae* within the phylum *Actinomycetota*. It is a common inhabitant of the gastrointestinal tract of humans and other mammals and is one of the most widely used probiotic species, particularly the subspecies *lactis*, valued for its acid and bile tolerance. Genomically, *B. animalis* has a relatively compact genome with a stable core and limited accessory content, reflecting its adaptation to the gut environment.
