*Moraxella osloensis* is an aerobic, oxidase-positive, Gram-negative coccobacillus in the family *Moraxellaceae*, commonly found on human skin and in the upper respiratory tract as a commensal. It is an uncommon but recognised opportunistic pathogen capable of causing bacteraemia, endocarditis, osteomyelitis, and central-line-associated infections, particularly in immunocompromised patients. The genome of *M. osloensis* (~2.5 Mb) is relatively poorly characterised but encodes type IV pili and lipooligosaccharide biosynthesis genes relevant to host colonisation.
