*Pseudomonas fluorescens* is a Gram-negative, aerobic rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, named for the fluorescent siderophores it produces under iron-limited conditions. It is a highly heterogeneous species complex commonly found in soil, water, and on plant surfaces, where it promotes plant growth and suppresses soil-borne pathogens. *P. fluorescens* sensu lato encompasses considerable phylogenomic diversity, and its large genome (~6.5 Mb) encodes versatile catabolic pathways, cold-adaptation genes, and secondary metabolite clusters relevant to both agriculture and biotechnology.
