*Xanthomonas translucens* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped plant pathogen of the family *Xanthomonadaceae* that causes bacterial leaf streak and black chaff on small-grain cereals including wheat, barley, and rye. Multiple pathovars infect different cereal hosts, and the disease can lead to substantial grain yield reductions, particularly under warm and humid conditions. *X. translucens* genomes encode type III effectors and, in some pathovars, TAL effectors, with comparative analyses revealing pathovar-specific accessory gene content that correlates with host adaptation.
