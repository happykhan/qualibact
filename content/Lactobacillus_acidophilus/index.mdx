*Lactobacillus acidophilus* is a homofermentative, Gram-positive rod in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, naturally inhabiting the human gastrointestinal and urogenital tracts. It is one of the most widely used probiotic species, valued for its acid tolerance, bile resistance, and ability to modulate gut microbiota. Genomically, *L. acidophilus* possesses a relatively compact genome (~2 Mb) enriched in genes for carbohydrate metabolism, surface-layer proteins, and bacteriocin production.
