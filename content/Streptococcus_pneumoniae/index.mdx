*Streptococcus pneumoniae* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic, lancet-shaped diplococcus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, commonly carried asymptomatically in the human nasopharynx. It is a major cause of community-acquired pneumonia, bacterial meningitis, otitis media, and sepsis worldwide, with the polysaccharide capsule being its principal virulence determinant and the basis for over 100 known serotypes. Genomically, *S. pneumoniae* is naturally transformable and highly recombinogenic, with an open pangenome that facilitates capsule switching, acquisition of antibiotic resistance, and vaccine escape.
