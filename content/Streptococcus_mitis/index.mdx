*Streptococcus mitis* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae* and a core member of the mitis group of viridans streptococci, closely related to *S. pneumoniae*. It is one of the most abundant commensals of the oral cavity and upper respiratory tract, but can cause infective endocarditis and bacteraemia, particularly in neutropenic patients. Genomically, *S. mitis* is highly recombinogenic with an open pangenome, frequently exchanging DNA with *S. pneumoniae*, complicating species-level identification and providing a reservoir for capsule and resistance gene transfer.
