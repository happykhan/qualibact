*Chlamydia trachomatis* is a Gram-negative, obligate intracellular bacterium of the family *Chlamydiaceae*, and the most common bacterial sexually transmitted pathogen worldwide. Different serovars cause trachoma (the leading infectious cause of blindness), urogenital infections, and lymphogranuloma venereum. *C. trachomatis* has a highly reduced genome of approximately 1.0 Mb, reflecting its dependence on host cell metabolites, along with a conserved virulence plasmid and a cryptic repertoire of polymorphic membrane proteins involved in host cell adhesion and immune evasion.
