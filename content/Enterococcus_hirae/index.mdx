*Enterococcus hirae* is a Gram-positive, catalase-negative coccus in the family *Enterococcaceae*, commonly found in the intestinal tract of animals, particularly poultry, swine, and cattle. It is a rare cause of human infection but has been reported in cases of bacteraemia, endocarditis, and urinary tract infections, mainly in immunocompromised individuals. *E. hirae* has been extensively studied as a model organism for cation transport and is notable for its well-characterised copper and potassium homeostasis systems.
