*Mycoplasma bovis* is a cell-wall-lacking, Gram-indeterminate bacterium in the family *Mycoplasmataceae* (class *Mollicutes*), and one of the most important mycoplasma pathogens of cattle worldwide. It causes pneumonia, mastitis, arthritis, and otitis media in calves and adult cattle, and is notoriously difficult to control due to the absence of a cell wall, which renders beta-lactam antibiotics ineffective. The genome of *M. bovis* (~1.0 Mb) is highly reduced and features an extensive repertoire of variable surface lipoproteins (Vsps) that mediate antigenic variation and immune evasion.
