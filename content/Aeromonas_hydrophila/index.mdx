*Aeromonas hydrophila* is a facultatively anaerobic, oxidase-positive Gram-negative rod in the family *Aeromonadaceae*, ubiquitous in freshwater, brackish water, and soil. It is an important pathogen of fish and amphibians and an opportunistic human pathogen capable of causing gastroenteritis, wound infections, and septicaemia, particularly in immunocompromised individuals. *A. hydrophila* possesses a versatile virulence arsenal including aerolysin, haemolysins, type III and type VI secretion systems, and multiple antimicrobial resistance determinants, supported by an open pangenome with extensive horizontal gene transfer.
