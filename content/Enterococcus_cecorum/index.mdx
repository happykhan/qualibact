*Enterococcus cecorum* is a facultatively anaerobic, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Enterococcaceae*, commonly found as a commensal in the intestinal tract of poultry and other birds. In recent decades it has emerged as a significant pathogen of broiler chickens, causing osteomyelitis, spondylitis, and femoral head necrosis with substantial economic impact on the poultry industry. Pathogenic strains of *E. cecorum* show genomic differences from commensal isolates, including acquisition of putative virulence loci and enhanced biofilm formation capacity.
