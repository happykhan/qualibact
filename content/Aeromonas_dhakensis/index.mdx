*Aeromonas dhakensis* is a facultatively anaerobic, oxidase-positive Gram-negative rod in the family *Aeromonadaceae*, found in warm freshwater and estuarine environments. Formerly classified within *A. hydrophila*, it is now recognised as one of the most virulent *Aeromonas* species, frequently associated with severe skin and soft-tissue infections, bacteraemia, and necrotising fasciitis, especially in immunocompromised hosts. *A. dhakensis* encodes a potent array of virulence factors including aerolysin, type III and type VI secretion systems, and lateral flagella, and its genome reflects a high degree of accessory gene content.
