*Mammaliicoccus sciuri* (formerly *Staphylococcus sciuri*) is a Gram-positive, coagulase-negative coccus in the family *Staphylococcaceae*, widely distributed in the skin and mucosal surfaces of animals and in environmental reservoirs. It is an opportunistic pathogen occasionally implicated in wound infections, endocarditis, and urinary tract infections, particularly in livestock and companion animals. *M. sciuri* is notable for carrying a chromosomal *mecA* homologue considered ancestral to the methicillin resistance determinant found in MRSA.
