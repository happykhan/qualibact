*Staphylococcus capitis* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, commonly found on human skin, particularly of the scalp and forehead. It is increasingly recognised as an opportunistic pathogen causing bloodstream infections and prosthetic device infections, especially in neonatal intensive care units where specific multidrug-resistant clones have disseminated globally. Genomically, *S. capitis* harbours a relatively compact genome with notable accessory elements carrying genes for biofilm formation and antimicrobial resistance.
