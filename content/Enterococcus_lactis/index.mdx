*Enterococcus lactis* is a Gram-positive, catalase-negative coccus in the family *Enterococcaceae*, recently separated from *Enterococcus faecium* as a distinct species based on genomic and phenotypic analyses. It is predominantly isolated from dairy products and fermented foods, and is generally considered to have low virulence potential. *E. lactis* typically lacks the hospital-adapted genomic markers found in clinical *E. faecium* clade A1 strains, and some strains are being evaluated for use as probiotics or starter cultures.
