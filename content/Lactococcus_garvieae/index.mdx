*Lactococcus garvieae* is a Gram-positive, non-motile coccus in the family *Streptococcaceae*, recognised as an important pathogen of farmed fish causing lactococcosis, and an emerging zoonotic agent in immunocompromised humans. It has been isolated from raw milk, seafood, and various environmental sources. The genome of *L. garvieae* (~2.0 Mb) encodes several virulence-associated factors, including a capsule locus, haemolysin, and adhesins, and shows notable intraspecific diversity.
