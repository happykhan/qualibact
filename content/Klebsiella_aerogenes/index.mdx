*Klebsiella aerogenes* (formerly *Enterobacter aerogenes*) is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic, encapsulated rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, found in the environment and the human gastrointestinal tract. It is a significant cause of nosocomial infections including pneumonia, urinary tract infections, bloodstream infections, and surgical-site infections, particularly in intensive care units. *K. aerogenes* possesses an inducible chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase and frequently acquires plasmid-mediated carbapenemases and extended-spectrum beta-lactamases, contributing to multidrug-resistant outbreaks.
