*Bacteroides cellulosilyticus* is an obligately anaerobic, non-spore-forming Gram-negative rod in the family *Bacteroidaceae*, found in the human gastrointestinal tract. As its name implies, it is capable of degrading cellulose and other complex plant cell-wall polysaccharides, and it has been used as a model for studying carbohydrate metabolism in the gut *Bacteroidota*. *B. cellulosilyticus* encodes one of the largest repertoires of polysaccharide utilisation loci among *Bacteroides* species, with over 100 PULs enabling the utilisation of a remarkably broad range of dietary and host glycans.
