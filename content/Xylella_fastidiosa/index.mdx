*Xylella fastidiosa* is a Gram-negative, fastidious, xylem-limited bacterium of the family *Xanthomonadaceae*, transmitted by xylem-feeding insect vectors such as sharpshooters and spittlebugs. It is the causal agent of numerous devastating plant diseases including Pierce's disease of grapevine, olive quick decline syndrome, and citrus variegated chlorosis, affecting over 500 plant species worldwide. *X. fastidiosa* has a relatively small genome (approximately 2.5 Mb) with significant inter-subspecies recombination, and its pathogenicity depends on biofilm formation, cell-cell signalling, and colonisation of host xylem vessels.
