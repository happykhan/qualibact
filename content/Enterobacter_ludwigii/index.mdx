*Enterobacter ludwigii* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, belonging to the *Enterobacter cloacae* complex and commonly found in clinical and environmental samples. It has been associated with nosocomial bacteraemia, urinary tract infections, and respiratory tract infections, though it is less frequently reported than other complex members. *E. ludwigii* carries a chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase and can acquire extended-spectrum resistance determinants, similar to other members of the complex.
