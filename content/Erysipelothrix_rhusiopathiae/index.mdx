*Erysipelothrix rhusiopathiae* is a Gram-positive, non-spore-forming, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Erysipelotrichaceae* within the class *Erysipelotrichia*. It is a zoonotic pathogen with a wide animal reservoir including swine, poultry, fish, and marine mammals, and causes erysipeloid in humans -- a localised cellulitis of the fingers and hands acquired through occupational exposure. In pigs it causes swine erysipelas, and *E. rhusiopathiae* produces a hyaluronidase and surface protective antigen (SpaA) that are important for evasion of phagocytosis and systemic virulence.
