*Propionibacterium freudenreichii* is a Gram-positive, non-motile, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Propionibacteriaceae*, best known as the principal ripening culture in Swiss-type cheese production, where its propionic acid fermentation generates the characteristic eyes and nutty flavour. It is generally recognised as safe (GRAS) and is also explored as a probiotic due to its production of short-chain fatty acids, vitamins B9 and B12, and bifidogenic compounds. *P. freudenreichii* genomes reveal adaptation to the dairy environment through extensive gene sets for lactate utilisation, stress tolerance, and surface-layer proteins involved in gut adhesion.
