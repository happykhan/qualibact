*Ruegeria pomeroyi* is a Gram-negative, aerobic alphaproteobacterium in the family *Rhodobacteraceae* (order *Rhodobacterales*), originally isolated from coastal seawater off the southeastern United States. It is a model organism for studying marine carbon and sulfur cycling, particularly the catabolism of dimethylsulfoniopropionate (DMSP), a key compound in the global sulfur cycle produced by marine phytoplankton. The genome of *R. pomeroyi* (~4.6 Mb) encodes diverse transporters and degradative pathways for algal osmolytes and has been instrumental in elucidating bacterium-phytoplankton interactions in the ocean.
