*Phocaeicola vulgatus* (formerly *Bacteroides vulgatus*) is an obligately anaerobic, Gram-negative rod of the family *Bacteroidaceae* and one of the most abundant bacterial species in the human colon. It plays a key role in dietary polysaccharide degradation and has been implicated in modulating mucosal immune responses, with some strains showing protective effects against colitis. *P. vulgatus* genomes are characterised by extensive polysaccharide utilisation loci (PULs), phase-variable capsular polysaccharide operons, and significant strain-level diversity in carbohydrate metabolism capacity.
