*Cronobacter turicensis* is a facultatively anaerobic, Gram-negative rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, first isolated from a fatal case of neonatal meningitis in Zurich. Like other *Cronobacter* species, it has been associated with severe infections in neonates, including necrotising enterocolitis and sepsis, often linked to contaminated powdered infant formula. The genome of *C. turicensis* encodes virulence factors including outer-membrane proteins, siderophores, and a type VI secretion system, and it carries a characteristic repA-type virulence plasmid.
