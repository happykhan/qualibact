*Bacteroides stercoris* is an obligately anaerobic, non-spore-forming Gram-negative rod in the family *Bacteroidaceae*, found as a commensal in the human gastrointestinal tract. It contributes to the breakdown of dietary polysaccharides and has been detected at altered abundances in metagenomic studies of metabolic and inflammatory conditions. *B. stercoris* possesses multiple polysaccharide utilisation loci and a genome typical of gut-adapted *Bacteroides*, though it has received less research attention than species such as *B. fragilis* or *B. thetaiotaomicron*.
