*Staphylococcus hyicus* is a coagulase-variable, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, primarily associated with pigs and other livestock. It is the causative agent of exudative epidermitis (greasy pig disease) in swine, mediated by exfoliative toxins that target desmoglein in the porcine epidermis, and can also cause mastitis in cattle. Genomically, *S. hyicus* carries a set of exfoliative toxin genes on mobile elements and shows moderate genomic diversity across host-adapted lineages.
