*Pseudomonas stutzeri* is a Gram-negative, aerobic, non-fluorescent rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, widely distributed in soil, water, and marine sediments. It is notable for its denitrifying ability and natural competence for DNA uptake, making it a model organism for studies of horizontal gene transfer and nitrogen cycling. *P. stutzeri* is a highly diverse genomospecies complex with at least 23 recognised genomovars, and although typically environmental, it has been occasionally implicated in opportunistic human infections.
