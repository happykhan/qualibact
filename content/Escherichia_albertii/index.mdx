*Escherichia albertii* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, initially misidentified as *Hafnia alvei* or atypical *Escherichia coli* before its formal species description. It is an emerging enteric pathogen causing diarrhoeal disease in humans and has been associated with outbreaks linked to contaminated food and water. *E. albertii* carries virulence genes homologous to those of enterohaemorrhagic and enteropathogenic *E. coli*, including intimin (*eae*) and cytolethal distending toxin (*cdt*), and its misidentification may lead to under-reporting.
