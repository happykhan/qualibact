*Staphylococcus schleiferi* is a Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae* that comprises both a coagulase-negative subspecies (*subsp. schleiferi*) and a coagulase-positive subspecies (*subsp. coagulans*). The coagulase-positive subspecies is a major cause of otitis externa and pyoderma in dogs, while the coagulase-negative form is an occasional human pathogen found in wound infections and bacteraemia. Genomically, *S. schleiferi* harbours genes for biofilm formation and antimicrobial resistance, with methicillin-resistant strains increasingly reported in veterinary settings.
