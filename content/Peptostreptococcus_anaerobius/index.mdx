*Peptostreptococcus anaerobius* is a Gram-positive, obligately anaerobic coccus of the family *Peptostreptococcaceae*, found as part of the commensal microbiota of the human oral cavity, gastrointestinal tract, and vagina. It is a clinically significant opportunistic pathogen involved in intra-abdominal abscesses, pelvic infections, and soft-tissue wounds, and has recently been implicated in the promotion of colorectal carcinogenesis. *P. anaerobius* genomes encode surface proteins that mediate attachment to colonic epithelium and activate pro-inflammatory signalling pathways, supporting its emerging role as an oncomicrobe.
