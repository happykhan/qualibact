*Enterobacter asburiae* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, found in clinical specimens, the environment, and the rhizosphere of plants. It is an occasional cause of healthcare-associated infections including bacteraemia, urinary tract infections, and wound infections. *E. asburiae* possesses a chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase and can acquire additional resistance determinants, contributing to multidrug-resistant phenotypes within the *Enterobacter cloacae* complex.
