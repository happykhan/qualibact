*Paraburkholderia xenovorans* is a Gram-negative, aerobic rod of the family *Burkholderiaceae*, originally isolated from a polychlorinated biphenyl (PCB)-contaminated landfill and formerly known as *Burkholderia xenovorans*. It is best known for its exceptional ability to degrade a broad range of aromatic pollutants, including PCBs, making it a model organism for bioremediation research. *P. xenovorans* strain LB400 possesses one of the largest known bacterial genomes (approximately 9.7 Mb) distributed across multiple replicons, encoding an extraordinary metabolic versatility with extensive catabolic gene clusters.
