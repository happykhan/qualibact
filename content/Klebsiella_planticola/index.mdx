*Klebsiella planticola* (reclassified as *Raoultella planticola*) is a Gram-negative, non-motile rod within the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, commonly isolated from water, soil, and plant surfaces. Although primarily an environmental organism, it has been reported as an opportunistic pathogen causing bacteraemia, pneumonia, and urinary tract infections in hospitalised patients. *R. planticola* possesses a relatively compact genome with notable metabolic versatility, including the ability to fix nitrogen and utilise a broad range of carbon sources.
