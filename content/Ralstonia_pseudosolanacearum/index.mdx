*Ralstonia pseudosolanacearum* is a Gram-negative, aerobic betaproteobacterium in the family *Burkholderiaceae* and a member of the *Ralstonia solanacearum* species complex. It is a devastating soilborne phytopathogen that causes bacterial wilt in a wide range of crops including tomato, potato, banana, and ginger, with a particularly broad host range in tropical and subtropical regions. *R. pseudosolanacearum* possesses a bipartite genome (chromosome and megaplasmid) with a large repertoire of type III effectors and shows considerable genomic plasticity driven by horizontal gene transfer.
