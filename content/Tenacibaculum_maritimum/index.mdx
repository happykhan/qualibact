*Tenacibaculum maritimum* is a Gram-negative, filamentous, gliding bacterium of the family *Flavobacteriaceae* within the order *Flavobacteriales*, found in marine environments. It is the primary causative agent of tenacibaculosis (marine flexibacteriosis), a devastating ulcerative skin disease affecting a wide range of farmed marine fish species worldwide. Genomically, *T. maritimum* possesses a moderately sized genome encoding extracellular proteases, gliding motility machinery, and type IX secretion system components that contribute to tissue adhesion and destruction.
