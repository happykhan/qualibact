*Burkholderia ubonensis* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting rod of the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc), originally isolated from soil and water in Thailand. It is predominantly an environmental species and is rarely associated with human infection, though occasional clinical isolates have been reported. Genomically, *B. ubonensis* exhibits a multi-chromosome architecture typical of the Bcc, with high genetic diversity among environmental isolates and a large accessory genome reflecting ecological adaptability.
