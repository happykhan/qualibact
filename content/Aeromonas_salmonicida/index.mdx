*Aeromonas salmonicida* is a facultatively anaerobic, non-motile Gram-negative rod in the family *Aeromonadaceae*, best known as the aetiological agent of furunculosis in salmonid fish. It is a major pathogen in aquaculture worldwide, causing systemic haemorrhagic disease with high mortality, and is subdivided into several subspecies with varying host ranges and virulence. *A. salmonicida* carries a type III secretion system, an A-layer surface array, and numerous plasmid-borne virulence and resistance genes, and its genome shows evidence of gene decay and insertion-sequence expansion compared to motile aeromonads.
