*Bordetella bronchiseptica* is a Gram-negative, aerobic coccobacillus of the family *Alcaligenaceae*, capable of infecting a broad range of mammalian hosts. It is a primary cause of kennel cough in dogs, atrophic rhinitis in pigs, and snuffles in rabbits, and occasionally causes respiratory infections in immunocompromised humans. *B. bronchiseptica* has a large genome (~5.3 Mb) compared to its host-restricted relatives *B. pertussis* and *B. parapertussis*, retaining extensive metabolic and regulatory gene repertoires that support its environmental persistence and wide host range.
