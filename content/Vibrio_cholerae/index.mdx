*Vibrio cholerae* is a Gram-negative, curved rod of the family *Vibrionaceae* and the aetiological agent of cholera, a severe diarrhoeal disease responsible for major pandemics throughout history. Toxigenic strains of serogroups O1 and O139 produce cholera toxin (CT) and the toxin-coregulated pilus (TCP), both encoded on mobile genetic elements, while non-toxigenic strains are common in estuarine environments. *V. cholerae* has a bipartite genome with extensive horizontal gene transfer, prophage content, and genomic islands that drive the emergence of new pandemic lineages.
