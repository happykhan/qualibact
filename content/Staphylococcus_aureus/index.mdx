*Staphylococcus aureus* is a coagulase-positive, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, commonly carried on the skin and in the nares of healthy individuals. It is one of the most clinically significant bacterial pathogens, causing a wide spectrum of diseases from skin infections and food poisoning to endocarditis, osteomyelitis, and sepsis, with methicillin-resistant strains (MRSA) posing a major public-health threat. Genomically, *S. aureus* features a highly conserved core genome alongside a rich mobile genetic element repertoire encoding toxins, immune evasion factors, and antimicrobial resistance determinants.
