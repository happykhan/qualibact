*Vibrio anguillarum* is a Gram-negative, curved rod-shaped bacterium of the family *Vibrionaceae* and a primary causative agent of vibriosis in marine and freshwater fish, leading to haemorrhagic septicaemia with high mortality. It inhabits temperate aquatic environments and is one of the most economically important pathogens in global aquaculture. The *V. anguillarum* genome typically includes a virulence plasmid (pJM1) encoding an iron-uptake siderophore system critical for pathogenicity, alongside chromosomally encoded toxins and adhesins.
