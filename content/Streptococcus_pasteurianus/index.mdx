*Streptococcus pasteurianus* is a Gram-positive coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, a member of the *Streptococcus bovis*/*equinus* complex (formerly *S. bovis* biotype II/2). It is a commensal of the human gastrointestinal tract and an agent of bacteraemia and endocarditis, sharing the clinically important association with colorectal disease seen in other bovis group species. Genomically, *S. pasteurianus* can be distinguished from related bovis complex members by whole-genome sequence analysis and harbours genes for bile tolerance and carbohydrate metabolism that reflect its intestinal niche.
