*Staphylococcus caprae* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, originally isolated from goat milk and also found on human skin. It is an opportunistic pathogen associated with bone and joint infections, prosthetic device infections, and bacteraemia, and has been linked to mastitis in small ruminants. Genomically, *S. caprae* carries a range of mobile genetic elements encoding antimicrobial resistance and biofilm-associated genes, reflecting its capacity to adapt to both animal and hospital environments.
