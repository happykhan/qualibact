*Bacillus mycoides* is an aerobic, endospore-forming Gram-positive rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, a member of the *Bacillus cereus* sensu lato group distinguished by its characteristic rhizoid colony morphology on agar plates. It is primarily a soil inhabitant and has been investigated as a plant growth-promoting rhizobacterium and biocontrol agent against certain phytopathogens. Genomically, *B. mycoides* shares the conserved chromosomal framework of the *B. cereus* group but typically lacks the major toxin-encoding plasmids associated with anthrax or emetic illness.
