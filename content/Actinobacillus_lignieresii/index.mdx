*Actinobacillus lignieresii* is a facultatively anaerobic Gram-negative coccobacillus in the family *Pasteurellaceae*, primarily inhabiting the oral cavity and upper respiratory tract of cattle and sheep. It is the causative agent of actinobacillosis ("wooden tongue"), a granulomatous infection of soft tissues in ruminants that can lead to significant economic losses in livestock production. Genomically, *A. lignieresii* possesses virulence-associated genes encoding RTX toxins and adhesins typical of the *Pasteurellaceae*, and occasional zoonotic infections in humans have been reported following animal bites or contact.
