*Enterobacter bugandensis* is a facultatively anaerobic, Gram-negative rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, first described from neonatal sepsis cases at a hospital in Kampala, Uganda. It has since been identified in nosocomial infections worldwide and was notably recovered from the International Space Station, highlighting its environmental persistence. *E. bugandensis* frequently harbours plasmid-borne carbapenemase and extended-spectrum beta-lactamase genes, making it a clinically concerning multidrug-resistant opportunistic pathogen.
