*Raoultella ornithinolytica* is a Gram-negative, encapsulated, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, closely related to *Klebsiella*, and found in aquatic environments, soil, and fish. It is an emerging opportunistic pathogen causing urinary tract infections, bloodstream infections, and biliary tract infections, particularly in elderly or immunocompromised patients. *R. ornithinolytica* is distinguished by its ability to decarboxylate ornithine and produce histamine, and its genome frequently carries acquired antimicrobial resistance determinants including extended-spectrum beta-lactamases.
