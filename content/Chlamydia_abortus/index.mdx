*Chlamydia abortus* is a Gram-negative, obligate intracellular bacterium of the family *Chlamydiaceae*, and a major cause of enzootic abortion (ovine enzootic abortion or OEA) in sheep and goats worldwide. It can also cause zoonotic infections in humans, particularly pregnant women exposed to infected livestock, leading to severe gestational complications. *C. abortus* has a highly reduced genome of approximately 1.1 Mb with a conserved chromosomal structure closely related to *C. psittaci*, from which it was reclassified, though it lacks the extensive outer membrane protein diversity seen in avian chlamydial lineages.
