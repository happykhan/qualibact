*Micrococcus luteus* is a Gram-positive, aerobic coccus in the family *Micrococcaceae* (order *Micrococcales*), characterised by its yellow-pigmented colonies. It is ubiquitous in the environment and a common commensal of human skin, but can occasionally act as an opportunistic pathogen causing bacteraemia, endocarditis, and catheter-related infections in immunocompromised individuals. The genome of *M. luteus* is GC-rich (approximately 73%) and relatively compact, encoding robust oxidative stress defences and UV-protective carotenoid biosynthesis pathways.
