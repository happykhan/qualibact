*Streptococcus canis* is a Gram-positive, beta-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, belonging to Lancefield group G. It is a commensal and pathogen of dogs and cats, causing skin infections, septicaemia, and necrotising fasciitis, and can occasionally be transmitted to humans, where it causes wound infections and bacteraemia. Genomically, *S. canis* carries virulence genes homologous to those in *S. pyogenes*, including M-like protein and streptolysin, reflecting shared pathogenic strategies within the pyogenic streptococci.
