*Edwardsiella piscicida* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod belonging to the family *Hafniaceae* within the order *Enterobacterales*, primarily recognised as a pathogen of farmed fish including tilapia, turbot, and channel catfish. It causes systemic edwardsiellosis characterised by haemorrhagic septicaemia and significant economic losses in aquaculture. Genomically, *E. piscicida* harbours a type III and type VI secretion system that are central to its intracellular survival and virulence.
