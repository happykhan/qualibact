*Proteus mirabilis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Morganellaceae*, well known for its characteristic swarming motility on solid surfaces. It is a common cause of complicated urinary tract infections, particularly catheter-associated infections, where it produces urease that alkalinises urine and promotes the formation of crystalline biofilms and struvite stones. The genome of *P. mirabilis* encodes extensive fimbrial operons, flagellar systems, and multiple iron-uptake pathways that collectively support its uropathogenic lifestyle.
