*Proteus vulgaris* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic, swarming rod in the family *Morganellaceae*, widely distributed in soil, water, and the intestinal tract of humans and animals. It is an opportunistic pathogen associated with urinary tract infections, wound infections, and nosocomial bacteraemia, particularly in debilitated or catheterised patients. *P. vulgaris* produces urease and has intrinsic resistance to several antibiotics; recent genomic reclassification has split the former species into a complex of closely related genomospecies.
