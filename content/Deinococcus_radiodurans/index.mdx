*Deinococcus radiodurans* is an aerobic, Gram-positive-staining (though with a complex, diderm-like cell envelope), non-motile coccus of the family *Deinococcaceae*, renowned as one of the most radiation-resistant organisms known. It can survive extreme doses of ionising radiation, desiccation, and oxidative stress, owing to highly efficient DNA repair mechanisms and a robust antioxidant defence system involving manganese complexes. The genome of *D. radiodurans* is organised into two chromosomes and two plasmids, and its DNA repair toolkit includes redundant recombination and excision repair pathways that enable rapid reassembly of shattered chromosomes.
