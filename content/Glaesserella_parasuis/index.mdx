*Glaesserella parasuis* (formerly *Haemophilus parasuis*) is a fastidious, Gram-negative, pleomorphic rod of the family *Pasteurellaceae*, found as a commensal of the upper respiratory tract of pigs. Virulent strains cause Glasser's disease, a systemic condition characterised by fibrinous polyserositis, polyarthritis, and meningitis, which represents a major economic burden in swine production. *G. parasuis* displays extensive serotype diversity (at least 15 serovars), and its genome shows significant strain-level variation in virulence-associated outer membrane proteins, polysaccharide loci, and trimeric autotransporters.
