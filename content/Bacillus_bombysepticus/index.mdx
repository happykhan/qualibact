*Bacillus bombysepticus* is an aerobic, endospore-forming Gram-positive rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, belonging to the *Bacillus cereus* sensu lato group. It is an insect pathogen originally isolated from diseased silkworms (*Bombyx mori*), in which it causes a lethal septicaemia, and has been studied as a potential biocontrol agent. Genomically, *B. bombysepticus* is closely related to *B. cereus* and *B. thuringiensis*, sharing a large chromosomal backbone and carrying insecticidal toxin genes and virulence factors on plasmids and pathogenicity islands.
