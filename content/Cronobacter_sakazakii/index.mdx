*Cronobacter sakazakii* is a Gram-negative, motile, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, found ubiquitously in the environment including dried foods such as powdered infant formula. It is a significant neonatal pathogen causing meningitis, necrotising enterocolitis, and bacteraemia with high mortality in premature and low-birth-weight infants. *C. sakazakii* possesses an array of virulence factors including outer-membrane proteins, a metalloprotease, and the ability to form resilient biofilms that enhance its persistence in desiccated environments.
