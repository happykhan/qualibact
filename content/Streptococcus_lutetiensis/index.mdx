*Streptococcus lutetiensis* is a Gram-positive coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, classified within the *Streptococcus bovis*/*equinus* complex (formerly *S. bovis* biotype II/1). It is a commensal of the human gastrointestinal tract and an emerging cause of bacteraemia and endocarditis, with a clinical association with colonic lesions similar to other members of the bovis group. Genomically, *S. lutetiensis* possesses a moderately sized genome and can be reliably distinguished from closely related bovis complex species by multilocus sequence analysis and whole-genome comparisons.
