*Stenotrophomonas maltophilia* is a non-fermenting, Gram-negative rod of the family *Lysobacteraceae* (order *Xanthomonadales*), ubiquitous in aqueous environments, soil, and the rhizosphere. It is an increasingly important opportunistic pathogen in hospitalised and immunocompromised patients, causing pneumonia, bloodstream infections, and catheter-related infections, with intrinsic resistance to many broad-spectrum antibiotics including carbapenems. Genomically, *S. maltophilia* is highly diverse, comprising multiple genomospecies with an open pangenome, chromosomally encoded beta-lactamases, and efflux pumps that underpin its multidrug-resistant phenotype.
