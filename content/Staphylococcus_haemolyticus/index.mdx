*Staphylococcus haemolyticus* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, commonly residing on human skin, particularly in the axillae and perineum. It is the second most frequently isolated coagulase-negative staphylococcus from blood cultures and is a significant cause of catheter-related bloodstream infections and endocarditis, frequently exhibiting multidrug resistance including heterogeneous resistance to vancomycin. Genomically, *S. haemolyticus* is notable for extensive insertion sequence content and a highly plastic genome that facilitates rapid acquisition of resistance determinants.
