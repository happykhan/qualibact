*Yersinia enterocolitica* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Yersiniaceae* and a common cause of foodborne gastroenteritis, mesenteric lymphadenitis, and terminal ileitis, particularly in cooler temperate regions. Transmission typically occurs through contaminated pork, milk, or water, and the organism can grow at refrigeration temperatures. Pathogenic biotypes of *Y. enterocolitica* carry a virulence plasmid (pYV) encoding a type III secretion system, while chromosomal pathogenicity islands contribute additional invasion and iron-uptake functions.
