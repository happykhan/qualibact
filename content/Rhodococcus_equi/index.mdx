*Rhodococcus equi* is a Gram-positive, aerobic coccobacillus of the family *Nocardiaceae* (order *Mycobacteriales*), found ubiquitously in soil and a significant pathogen of foals, in which it causes severe pyogranulomatous pneumonia. It is also an opportunistic pathogen in immunocompromised humans, particularly those with HIV/AIDS, causing cavitary lung disease resembling tuberculosis. Virulence in *R. equi* is conferred by a large conjugative plasmid carrying the *vapA* or *vapB* pathogenicity island, and its genome encodes an extensive lipid metabolism network reflecting its mycolic-acid-containing cell envelope.
