*Burkholderia dolosa* is a non-fermenting, Gram-negative rod of the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc), first described from respiratory specimens of cystic fibrosis patients. It is an opportunistic pathogen associated with epidemic outbreaks in CF centres and can cause severe necrotising pneumonia and septicaemia. Genomic analyses of *B. dolosa* reveal a multi-chromosome architecture with evidence of within-patient adaptive evolution, including mutations affecting lipopolysaccharide biosynthesis and antibiotic resistance.
