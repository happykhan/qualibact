*Kingella kingae* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic coccobacillus in the family *Neisseriaceae* within the order *Neisseriales*, and a commensal of the oropharynx in young children. It is the leading cause of osteoarticular infections (septic arthritis, osteomyelitis, and spondylodiscitis) in children aged 6 months to 4 years, and is also a member of the HACEK group of endocarditis-causing organisms. *K. kingae* employs a type IV pilus for epithelial colonisation and produces a potent RTX cytotoxin that facilitates tissue invasion.
