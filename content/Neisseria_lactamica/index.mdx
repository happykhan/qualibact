*Neisseria lactamica* is a Gram-negative, oxidase-positive diplococcus in the family *Neisseriaceae*, distinguished by its ability to utilise lactose, and is a common commensal of the nasopharynx in young children. Colonisation with *N. lactamica* is associated with the development of natural immunity to *Neisseria meningitidis*, and the species has been investigated as a basis for novel mucosal vaccine strategies. Genomically, *N. lactamica* shares substantial core-genome similarity with *N. meningitidis* but lacks key virulence loci such as the capsule biosynthesis genes.
