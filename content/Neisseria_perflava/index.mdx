*Neisseria perflava* is a Gram-negative, oxidase-positive diplococcus in the family *Neisseriaceae*, commonly inhabiting the human oropharynx as a commensal. It is generally non-pathogenic but has been implicated in rare cases of endocarditis, meningitis, and respiratory infections, mainly in patients with underlying conditions. *N. perflava* is part of a closely related cluster of pharyngeal commensal *Neisseria* species and participates in interspecies horizontal gene transfer that can disseminate antimicrobial resistance genes across the genus.
