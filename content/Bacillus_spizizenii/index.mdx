*Bacillus spizizenii* is an aerobic, endospore-forming Gram-positive rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, closely related to *B. subtilis* and formerly classified as *B. subtilis* subsp. *spizizenii*. It is found primarily in soil and has been used as a laboratory model organism, with several widely used "B. subtilis" strains (e.g., W23) now reclassified as *B. spizizenii*. Genomically, *B. spizizenii* differs from *B. subtilis* sensu stricto in its cell-wall teichoic acid biosynthesis genes and ribosomal protein sequences, while retaining the natural competence and sporulation capabilities characteristic of the *B. subtilis* species complex.
