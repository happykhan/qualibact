*Staphylococcus coagulans* is a coagulase-positive, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, typically arranged in clusters. It has been isolated from animals and animal-derived products, and despite its coagulase-positive phenotype it is distinct from *Staphylococcus aureus* based on molecular typing. *S. coagulans* has a moderately sized genome and its coagulase gene shows sequence divergence from the classical *S. aureus* coagulase, aiding species-level differentiation.
