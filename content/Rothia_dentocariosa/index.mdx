*Rothia dentocariosa* is a Gram-positive, facultatively anaerobic, pleomorphic rod or coccobacillus in the family *Micrococcaceae* (order *Micrococcales*), and a normal inhabitant of the human oral cavity and upper respiratory tract. Although generally commensal, it can act as an opportunistic pathogen causing endocarditis, prosthetic joint infections, and bacteraemia, particularly in immunocompromised individuals or those with poor dentition. *R. dentocariosa* produces biofilms on dental surfaces and possesses a moderately sized genome with genes involved in carbohydrate metabolism and adhesion to host tissues.
