*Treponema pallidum* is a Gram-negative, spirochete bacterium of the family *Treponemataceae*, obligately parasitic and not cultivable on artificial media. Subspecies *pallidum* is the causative agent of syphilis, a sexually transmitted infection with multistage clinical manifestations that remains a major global public health concern. Genomically, *T. pallidum* has a highly reduced genome (~1.14 Mb) with minimal metabolic capacity, extensive reliance on host-derived nutrients, and remarkably low genetic diversity, though recombination at the *tprK* locus enables antigenic variation and immune evasion.
