*Neisseria flavescens* is a Gram-negative, oxidase-positive diplococcus in the family *Neisseriaceae*, typically found as a commensal of the human oropharynx and nasopharynx. It is generally non-pathogenic but has rarely been reported in cases of meningitis, septicaemia, and endocarditis, primarily in immunocompromised hosts. *N. flavescens* participates actively in horizontal gene transfer within the genus, and its genome can serve as a reservoir of antimicrobial resistance determinants, including penA alleles, that may transfer to pathogenic *Neisseria* species.
