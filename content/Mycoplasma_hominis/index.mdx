*Mycoplasma hominis* is a cell-wall-lacking, Gram-indeterminate bacterium in the family *Mycoplasmataceae* (class *Mollicutes*), commonly found in the urogenital tract where it can exist as a commensal or as an opportunistic pathogen. It is associated with bacterial vaginosis, pelvic inflammatory disease, postpartum fever, and neonatal infections, and can cause extragenital infections such as wound infections and septic arthritis in immunocompromised individuals. The genome of *M. hominis* (~0.67 Mb) is intrinsically resistant to macrolides due to a naturally occurring 23S rRNA mutation, and encodes a variable surface antigen system that facilitates immune evasion.
