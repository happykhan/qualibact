*Streptomyces anthocyanicus* is a Gram-positive, filamentous actinobacterium of the family *Streptomycetaceae*, characterised by the production of dark purple to violet pigments resembling anthocyanins. It is typically isolated from soil environments and, like other streptomycetes, forms branching substrate and aerial mycelia that differentiate into spore chains. *S. anthocyanicus* possesses a large, high-G+C linear genome rich in biosynthetic gene clusters for secondary metabolites, including polyketides and non-ribosomal peptides.
