*Listeria ivanovii* is a Gram-positive, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Listeriaceae*, and the only other *Listeria* species besides *L. monocytogenes* that is consistently pathogenic, though almost exclusively in ruminants. It causes abortion and septicaemia in sheep and cattle and is only rarely associated with human disease. The genome of *L. ivanovii* (~2.9 Mb) carries a distinct species-specific pathogenicity island (LIPI-2) encoding sphingomyelinase and internalin-like genes not found in *L. monocytogenes*.
