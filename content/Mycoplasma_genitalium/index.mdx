*Mycoplasma genitalium* is a cell-wall-lacking, Gram-indeterminate bacterium in the family *Mycoplasmataceae* (class *Mollicutes*), and a sexually transmitted pathogen causing urethritis in men and cervicitis, pelvic inflammatory disease, and adverse pregnancy outcomes in women. It possesses one of the smallest genomes of any self-replicating organism (~0.58 Mb, ~475 protein-coding genes), making it a foundational model for minimal genome studies and synthetic biology. Rapidly increasing macrolide and fluoroquinolone resistance in *M. genitalium* has made resistance-guided therapy essential in clinical management.
