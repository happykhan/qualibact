*Vibrio fluvialis* is a Gram-negative, halophilic, curved rod of the family *Vibrionaceae*, found in marine, estuarine, and brackish-water environments worldwide. It is an emerging human pathogen that causes cholera-like gastroenteritis, wound infections, and occasional bacteraemia, often associated with consumption of raw or undercooked seafood. *V. fluvialis* has a two-chromosome genome and carries a range of virulence factors including a type VI secretion system, haemolysins, and metalloprotease genes, along with increasing reports of multidrug resistance.
