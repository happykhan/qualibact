*Trueperella pyogenes* is a Gram-positive, non-motile, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Actinomycetaceae*, previously classified as *Arcanobacterium pyogenes*. It is a commensal of the mucosal surfaces of livestock and a major opportunistic pathogen of cattle, swine, and small ruminants, causing liver abscesses, mastitis, metritis, and suppurative infections. *T. pyogenes* genomes encode key virulence factors including the cholesterol-dependent cytolysin pyolysin, collagen-binding proteins, and fimbriae that facilitate tissue colonisation and abscess formation.
