*Agrobacterium pusense* is an aerobic Gram-negative rod in the family *Rhizobiaceae*, originally isolated from the rhizosphere of *Cicer arietinum* (chickpea) in Pune, India. Unlike the closely related *A. tumefaciens*, it typically lacks the Ti plasmid and is non-pathogenic to plants, though it has been sporadically reported from clinical specimens including bloodstream infections. *A. pusense* has a multipartite genome and shares substantial core gene content with other members of the *Agrobacterium* biovar 1 group, with its clinical relevance still being elucidated.
