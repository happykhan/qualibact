*Pediococcus pentosaceus* is a Gram-positive, homofermentative lactic acid coccus in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, typically arranged in tetrads, and widely used in food fermentation. It is important in the production of fermented vegetables, sausages, and silage, where it contributes to preservation through rapid acid production and bacteriocin synthesis. *P. pentosaceus* has a relatively compact genome (~1.8 Mb) and is increasingly studied as a candidate probiotic owing to its acid tolerance, bile resistance, and ability to produce antimicrobial peptides such as pediocin.
