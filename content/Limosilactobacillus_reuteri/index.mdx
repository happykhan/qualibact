*Limosilactobacillus reuteri* (formerly *Lactobacillus reuteri*) is an obligately heterofermentative, Gram-positive rod in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, notable for its broad vertebrate host range spanning humans, rodents, pigs, and poultry. It is one of the best-characterised probiotic species and produces the antimicrobial compound reuterin from glycerol fermentation. The genome of *L. reuteri* (~2.0 Mb) exhibits strong host-specific lineage structure, with distinct genomic clades reflecting long-term co-evolution with different vertebrate hosts.
