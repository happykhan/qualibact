*Lactobacillus paragasseri* is a Gram-positive, homofermentative rod in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, recently separated from *Lactobacillus gasseri* on the basis of whole-genome analyses. It inhabits the human gastrointestinal and vaginal tracts and shares many probiotic-associated traits with its close relative. *L. paragasseri* is genomically distinguished by specific accessory gene clusters, and many historical *L. gasseri* reference strains have been reclassified to this species.
