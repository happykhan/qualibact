*Francisella tularensis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively intracellular, non-motile coccobacillus in the family *Francisellaceae*, and the causative agent of tularaemia, a zoonotic disease transmitted by arthropod bites, contact with infected animals, or inhalation of aerosols. It is one of the most infectious bacterial pathogens known, with as few as 10 organisms sufficient to cause disease, and is classified as a Tier 1 select agent due to its bioterrorism potential. *F. tularensis* has a relatively small genome (~1.9 Mb) with a duplicated pathogenicity island encoding a type VI-like secretion system essential for intramacrophage survival.
