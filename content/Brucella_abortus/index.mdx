*Brucella abortus* is a Gram-negative, facultatively intracellular coccobacillus of the family *Brucellaceae* within the order *Hyphomicrobiales*, and the principal cause of bovine brucellosis worldwide. In cattle it causes late-term abortion and infertility, and in humans it produces a systemic febrile illness acquired through contact with infected animals or consumption of unpasteurised dairy products. *B. abortus* has a bipartite genome of two circular chromosomes totalling approximately 3.3 Mb, with very high sequence conservation across biovars reflecting its clonal evolution and obligate intracellular lifestyle.
