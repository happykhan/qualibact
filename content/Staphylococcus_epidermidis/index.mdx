*Staphylococcus epidermidis* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae* and the most abundant commensal bacterium on human skin and mucous membranes. It is a leading cause of nosocomial infections associated with indwelling medical devices, including central venous catheters and prosthetic joints, primarily through robust biofilm formation. Genomically, *S. epidermidis* displays considerable strain-level diversity, with an open pangenome enriched in mobile genetic elements carrying antibiotic resistance and biofilm-related genes.
