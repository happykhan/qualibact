*Klebsiella pasteurii* is a Gram-negative, non-motile rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, recently delineated as a novel species within the *Klebsiella oxytoca* species complex. It has been recovered from both clinical and environmental sources and is frequently misidentified as *K. oxytoca* by routine phenotypic methods. Genomic analyses place *K. pasteurii* as a distinct clade, and certain strains harbour genes encoding cytotoxin production and antimicrobial resistance.
