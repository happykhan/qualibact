*Borreliella burgdorferi* is a Gram-negative spirochaete of the family *Borreliaceae* and the primary causative agent of Lyme disease in North America. It is transmitted to humans through the bite of infected *Ixodes* ticks and can cause multisystem disease affecting the skin, joints, heart, and nervous system. *B. burgdorferi* has a unique segmented genome comprising a linear chromosome and over twenty linear and circular plasmids, which encode a diverse repertoire of variable surface antigens essential for tick-mammal transmission cycling.
