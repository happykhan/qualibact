*Citrobacter koseri* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, found in soil, water, and the intestinal flora of humans. It is clinically notable for its propensity to cause neonatal meningitis and brain abscesses, with a characteristic tropism for the central nervous system in neonates and young infants. *C. koseri* has a genome of approximately 4.7 Mb and, unlike *C. freundii*, lacks a chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase, though it can acquire plasmid-mediated resistance elements.
