*Stutzerimonas stutzeri* (formerly *Pseudomonas stutzeri*) is a non-fluorescent, Gram-negative rod of the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, widely distributed in soil, water, and marine sediments. It is best known for its remarkable metabolic versatility, including complete denitrification, natural competence for DNA uptake, and the ability to degrade a wide range of aromatic compounds and, in some strains, fix nitrogen. *S. stutzeri* genomes display high intraspecific diversity organised into multiple genomovars, with a large accessory genome reflecting adaptation to diverse environmental niches.
