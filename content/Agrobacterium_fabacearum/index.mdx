*Agrobacterium fabacearum* is an aerobic Gram-negative rod in the family *Rhizobiaceae* within the order *Hyphomicrobiales*, isolated from root nodules and the rhizosphere of leguminous plants. It is a recently described species closely related to *A. tumefaciens*, but it is generally non-tumorigenic and associated with beneficial plant-microbe interactions. *A. fabacearum* possesses a multipartite genome typical of the rhizobia, including a circular chromosome and one or more large plasmids, and shares conserved genes for rhizosphere colonisation with related agrobacteria.
