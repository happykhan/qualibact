*Clostridium beijerinckii* is an anaerobic, Gram-positive, spore-forming rod of the family *Clostridiaceae*, widely distributed in soil and sediments. It is best known for its industrial significance as a solventogenic bacterium capable of producing acetone, butanol, and ethanol (ABE fermentation) from a broad range of carbohydrate substrates. The genome of *C. beijerinckii* is approximately 6 Mb and encodes a large solventogenic megaplasmid along with extensive carbohydrate catabolism and sporulation gene clusters.
