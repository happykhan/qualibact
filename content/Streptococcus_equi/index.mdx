*Streptococcus equi* is a Gram-positive, beta-haemolytic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, belonging to Lancefield group C. Subspecies *equi* is the causative agent of strangles, a highly contagious upper respiratory infection of horses characterised by abscessation of the lymph nodes of the head and neck. Genomically, *S. equi* subsp. *equi* evolved from the more genetically diverse subspecies *zooepidemicus* through genome reduction and acquisition of specific virulence factors, including the superantigen SePE and the antiphagocytic SeM protein.
