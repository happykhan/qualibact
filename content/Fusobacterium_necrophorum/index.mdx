*Fusobacterium necrophorum* is a Gram-negative, obligately anaerobic, pleomorphic rod in the family *Fusobacteriaceae*, commonly found in the oropharynx and gastrointestinal tract of humans and animals. It is the primary causative agent of Lemierre syndrome (postanginal septic thrombophlebitis of the internal jugular vein), as well as peritonsillar abscesses, liver abscesses, and other invasive infections, predominantly in previously healthy young adults. *F. necrophorum* produces the leukotoxin LktA, a key virulence factor that targets neutrophils and is essential for tissue invasion and systemic dissemination.
