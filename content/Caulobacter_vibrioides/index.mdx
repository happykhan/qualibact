*Caulobacter vibrioides* (also known as *C. crescentus*) is a Gram-negative, stalked alphaproteobacterium of the family *Caulobacteraceae*, found in oligotrophic freshwater environments. It is a premier model organism for the study of bacterial cell cycle regulation, asymmetric cell division, and polar morphogenesis. The well-annotated 4.0 Mb genome of *C. vibrioides* has been instrumental in elucidating master regulators of cell differentiation such as CtrA, DnaA, and GcrA.
