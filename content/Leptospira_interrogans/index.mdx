*Leptospira interrogans* is a motile spirochaete with a Gram-negative-like cell envelope in the family *Leptospiraceae*, and is the most common causative agent of leptospirosis, a globally distributed zoonosis. Infection occurs through contact with water or soil contaminated by the urine of carrier animals, particularly rodents, and can range from mild febrile illness to severe multiorgan failure (Weil's disease). The bipartite genome of *L. interrogans* (~4.6 Mb) encodes a rich complement of lipoproteins, signalling systems, and motility genes that underpin its ability to colonise diverse mammalian hosts.
