*Raoultella planticola* is a Gram-negative, encapsulated, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, originally isolated from water and botanical environments and formerly classified within *Klebsiella*. It is an uncommon but increasingly recognised opportunistic pathogen associated with bloodstream infections, pneumonia, and urinary tract infections in hospitalised patients. *R. planticola* can fix nitrogen under anaerobic conditions and its genome shares extensive synteny with *Klebsiella*, though it is distinguished by specific metabolic capabilities and capsular locus variants.
