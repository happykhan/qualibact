*Vibrio alginolyticus* is a halophilic, Gram-negative curved rod belonging to the family *Vibrionaceae*, widely distributed in warm coastal and estuarine waters worldwide. It is an opportunistic pathogen in humans, causing wound infections, otitis externa, and occasional bacteraemia, and is also a significant pathogen of marine fish and shellfish. *V. alginolyticus* genomes carry multiple virulence-associated gene clusters, including those encoding haemolysins and type III secretion systems, reflecting its dual role as an environmental organism and emerging pathogen.
