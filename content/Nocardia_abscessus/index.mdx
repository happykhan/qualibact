*Nocardia abscessus* is a partially acid-fast, Gram-positive filamentous actinobacterium of the family *Nocardiaceae*, found in soil and aquatic environments. It is an opportunistic human pathogen that predominantly causes pulmonary nocardiosis in immunocompromised patients, as well as cutaneous and disseminated infections. *N. abscessus* genomes are relatively large (approximately 7--8 Mb) and encode diverse secondary metabolite gene clusters and efflux pumps, contributing to intrinsic resistance to multiple antimicrobial agents.
