*Brucella canis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively intracellular coccobacillus of the family *Brucellaceae*, primarily causing brucellosis in dogs, characterised by reproductive failure, abortion, and discospondylitis. It can also infect humans through direct contact with infected canine reproductive fluids and tissues, causing a chronic febrile illness that is often difficult to diagnose serologically. *B. canis* has a bipartite genome highly similar to other *Brucella* species, but is distinguished by a rough lipopolysaccharide phenotype due to disruptions in LPS biosynthesis genes, which influences its virulence and immune evasion strategies.
