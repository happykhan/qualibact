*Acinetobacter johnsonii* is a non-fermenting, aerobic Gram-negative coccobacillus in the family *Moraxellaceae*, commonly isolated from human skin, animal products, and wastewater treatment systems. Although generally considered a commensal of low virulence, *A. johnsonii* has been occasionally implicated in catheter-related bloodstream infections and other opportunistic infections in immunocompromised patients. Its genome is notable for carrying a variety of antimicrobial resistance determinants, including genes encoding aminoglycoside-modifying enzymes that can be transferred to other *Acinetobacter* species.
