*Ligilactobacillus salivarius* (formerly *Lactobacillus salivarius*) is a homofermentative, Gram-positive rod in the family *Lactobacillaceae*, commonly isolated from the human oral cavity, gastrointestinal tract, and breast milk. It has attracted interest as a probiotic due to its ability to produce bacteriocins active against *Listeria* and other pathogens. The genome of *L. salivarius* (~1.8-2.1 Mb) typically includes one or more megaplasmids that carry key bacteriocin and carbohydrate metabolism loci.
