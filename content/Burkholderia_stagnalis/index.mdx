*Burkholderia stagnalis* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting rod belonging to the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc), first described from environmental water samples and subsequently from clinical sources. It is considered an opportunistic pathogen with the potential to cause respiratory infections in cystic fibrosis patients. Like other Bcc members, *B. stagnalis* possesses a multi-replicon genome with broad metabolic capabilities and intrinsic resistance to multiple classes of antibiotics.
