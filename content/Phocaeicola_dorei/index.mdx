*Phocaeicola dorei* (formerly *Bacteroides dorei*) is a Gram-negative, obligately anaerobic, non-motile rod in the family *Bacteroidaceae* (phylum *Bacteroidota*), and a prevalent member of the human gut microbiota. It has been associated with modulation of host immune responses and has attracted interest for its potential role in early-life microbiome development and autoimmune disease risk. *P. dorei* carries a sizeable genome with numerous polysaccharide utilisation loci and conjugative transposons, and shows notable strain-level diversity in capsular polysaccharide biosynthesis genes.
