*Citrobacter portucalensis* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, first described from clinical and environmental isolates in Portugal. It belongs to the *Citrobacter freundii* complex and has been increasingly recognised as an opportunistic pathogen in bloodstream and urinary tract infections, often in nosocomial settings. *C. portucalensis* carries a chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase and has been reported to harbour plasmid-mediated carbapenemase genes, contributing to multidrug-resistant phenotypes.
