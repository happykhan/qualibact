*Rhizobium leguminosarum* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped alphaproteobacterium of the family *Rhizobiaceae*, widely found in temperate soils. It forms nitrogen-fixing symbioses with leguminous plants such as peas, clovers, and beans by inducing root nodules and converting atmospheric dinitrogen to ammonia. *R. leguminosarum* has a multipartite genome comprising a chromosome and several large plasmids, including the symbiosis plasmid that carries the *nod* and *nif* gene clusters essential for nodulation and nitrogen fixation.
