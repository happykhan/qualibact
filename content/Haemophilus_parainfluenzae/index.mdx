*Haemophilus parainfluenzae* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic coccobacillus in the family *Pasteurellaceae*, requiring only the V (NAD) growth factor, and one of the most abundant colonisers of the human oropharynx and dental plaque. Although primarily a commensal, it is a member of the HACEK group and an occasional cause of endocarditis, bacteraemia, pneumonia, and other invasive infections. *H. parainfluenzae* is taxonomically heterogeneous, and genomic analyses suggest that it may encompass multiple distinct species or genomospecies.
