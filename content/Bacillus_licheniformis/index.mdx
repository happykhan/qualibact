*Bacillus licheniformis* is a facultatively anaerobic, endospore-forming Gram-positive rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, widely distributed in soil, plant material, and bird feathers. It is industrially important as a prolific producer of enzymes (proteases, amylases), antibiotics (bacitracin, lichenicidin), and biosurfactants, and holds GRAS (Generally Recognised As Safe) status for many applications. *B. licheniformis* has a genome of approximately 4.2 Mb with extensive secondary metabolite biosynthetic gene clusters, and while generally regarded as safe, it has been rarely associated with foodborne illness and opportunistic infections.
