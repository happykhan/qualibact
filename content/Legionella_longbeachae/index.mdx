*Legionella longbeachae* is an aerobic, Gram-negative rod in the family *Legionellaceae*, primarily associated with potting mix and composted soil rather than aquatic environments. It is a leading cause of Legionnaires' disease in Australia and New Zealand, where gardening-related exposures predominate. The genome of *L. longbeachae* (~3.9 Mb) contains a large plasmid and multiple type IV secretion system effectors, but differs from *L. pneumophila* in its repertoire of surface structures and environmental adaptation genes.
