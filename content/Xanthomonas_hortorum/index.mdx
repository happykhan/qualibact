*Xanthomonas hortorum* is a Gram-negative, aerobic rod of the family *Xanthomonadaceae*, comprising several pathovars that cause bacterial leaf spot and blight on a wide range of horticultural crops including lettuce, carrot, pelargonium, and ivy. Like other xanthomonads, it produces yellow xanthomonadin pigments and xanthan gum, and relies on a type III secretion system to deliver effector proteins into host cells. *X. hortorum* has a genome of approximately 5 Mb with a diverse effector repertoire that varies among pathovars and underlies host specificity.
