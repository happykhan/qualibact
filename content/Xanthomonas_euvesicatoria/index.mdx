*Xanthomonas euvesicatoria* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped plant pathogen in the family *Xanthomonadaceae* and one of the primary agents of bacterial spot disease on pepper and tomato worldwide. It was formerly classified within the *Xanthomonas axonopodis* complex and is distinguished by a specific repertoire of type III effectors and transcription activator-like (TAL) effectors. *X. euvesicatoria* genomes show high plasticity, with effector gene gain and loss events driving race structure and the ability to overcome host resistance.
