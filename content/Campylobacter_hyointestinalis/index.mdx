*Campylobacter hyointestinalis* is a microaerophilic, Gram-negative, curved rod of the family *Campylobacteraceae*, primarily associated with proliferative enteritis in pigs and cattle. It can occasionally cause gastroenteritis in humans and has been isolated from various animal reservoirs including poultry and deer. *C. hyointestinalis* has a relatively small genome typical of campylobacters, with limited metabolic versatility and notable variability in surface-exposed glycosylation loci among strains.
