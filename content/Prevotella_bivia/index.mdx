*Prevotella bivia* is a Gram-negative, obligately anaerobic rod in the family *Prevotellaceae*, commonly found in the female genital tract as part of the normal vaginal microbiota. It is a frequent isolate from polymicrobial pelvic infections, including pelvic inflammatory disease, endometritis, and tubo-ovarian abscesses, often in synergy with other anaerobes and facultative organisms. *P. bivia* produces proteases and can deconjugate bile acids, and its genome reflects adaptation to the nutrient-rich mucosal environment of the reproductive tract.
