*Staphylococcus chromogenes* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, commonly found on the skin and mucous membranes of ruminants. It is the most frequently isolated coagulase-negative staphylococcal species from bovine intramammary infections, where it typically causes mild subclinical mastitis. Genomically, *S. chromogenes* possesses a moderately sized genome with genes related to adhesion, biofilm formation, and fatty acid metabolism that are thought to contribute to its niche adaptation in the bovine udder.
