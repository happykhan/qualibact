*Enterococcus durans* is a Gram-positive, catalase-negative coccus in the family *Enterococcaceae*, found in the intestinal tract of humans and animals as well as in dairy products and fermented foods. It is an infrequent but recognised cause of opportunistic infections including endocarditis, urinary tract infections, and bacteraemia, predominantly in hospitalised patients. *E. durans* can acquire vancomycin resistance genes and other mobile resistance elements, although it is less commonly multidrug-resistant than *E. faecium*.
