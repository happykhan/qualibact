*Citrobacter amalonaticus* is a facultatively anaerobic, Gram-negative rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, distinguished from other citrobacters by its inability to utilise malonate. It is found in the human gastrointestinal tract and environmental sources and can act as an opportunistic pathogen, causing urinary tract infections, bacteraemia, and neonatal meningitis. Genomically, *C. amalonaticus* shares extensive synteny with other *Citrobacter* species but carries variable accessory elements including antibiotic resistance plasmids.
