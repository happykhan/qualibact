*Ureaplasma urealyticum* is a cell-wall-lacking bacterium of the class *Mollicutes* and family *Mycoplasmataceae*, distinguished by its urease-dependent energy metabolism. It colonises the human urogenital tract and is associated with non-gonococcal urethritis, adverse pregnancy outcomes, and invasive infections in immunocompromised neonates. *U. urealyticum* possesses a minimal genome (approximately 0.87 Mb) with reduced biosynthetic pathways, and its ten serovars display phase-variable surface antigen genes that may contribute to immune evasion.
