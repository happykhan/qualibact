*Neisseria bergeri* is a Gram-negative, oxidase-positive diplococcus in the family *Neisseriaceae*, recently described as a commensal species of the human upper respiratory tract. It was previously classified within *Neisseria polysaccharea* or related unnamed genomospecies before being resolved as a distinct taxon by whole-genome analyses. *N. bergeri* lacks the major virulence factors of pathogenic *Neisseria* species but shares extensive genetic exchange with other members of the genus through natural transformation.
