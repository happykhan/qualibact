*Corynebacterium pseudodiphtheriticum* is an aerobic, Gram-positive, non-sporulating rod of the family *Corynebacteriaceae*, commonly found as a commensal of the human nasopharynx and skin. Although generally considered non-toxigenic, it has been increasingly recognised as an opportunistic pathogen causing respiratory tract infections, particularly pneumonia in elderly and immunocompromised patients. Genomically, *C. pseudodiphtheriticum* lacks the diphtheria toxin gene present in *C. diphtheriae* but carries adhesion and immune-modulation factors relevant to its mucosal niche.
