*Paenibacillus larvae* is a Gram-positive, spore-forming rod in the family *Paenibacillaceae* and the causative agent of American foulbrood, the most devastating bacterial disease of honeybee larvae worldwide. The highly resistant endospores can persist in hive materials for decades, making eradication extremely difficult once an apiary is infected. Genomically, *P. larvae* encodes a diverse arsenal of virulence factors including secondary metabolites, toxins, and chitin-degrading enzymes that facilitate larval tissue degradation.
