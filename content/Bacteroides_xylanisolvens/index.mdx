*Bacteroides xylanisolvens* is an obligately anaerobic, non-spore-forming Gram-negative rod in the family *Bacteroidaceae*, isolated from human faeces and closely related to *B. ovatus*. As its name indicates, it specialises in the degradation of xylan and other hemicellulosic substrates, contributing to short-chain fatty acid production in the colon. *B. xylanisolvens* has received attention for its immunomodulatory properties, and an inactivated strain expressing blood-group B antigen has been evaluated for enzymatic conversion of blood types, highlighting the biotechnological potential of its surface glycobiology.
