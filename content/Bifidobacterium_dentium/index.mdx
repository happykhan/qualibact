*Bifidobacterium dentium* is an anaerobic, Gram-positive rod of the family *Bifidobacteriaceae*, originally isolated from human dental caries. Unlike most bifidobacteria, which are considered beneficial gut commensals, *B. dentium* is associated with oral cavity infections and has been linked to cariogenic activity through its capacity to produce acid from a wide range of carbohydrates. Its genome encodes an unusually large repertoire of carbohydrate-utilisation loci compared to other bifidobacteria, underpinning its ecological niche in the oral environment.
