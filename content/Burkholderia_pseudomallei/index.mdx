*Burkholderia pseudomallei* is a Gram-negative, motile, non-fermenting rod of the family *Burkholderiaceae* and the causative agent of melioidosis, a severe tropical infection endemic to Southeast Asia and northern Australia. It is a saprophytic soil and water organism that can infect humans through percutaneous inoculation, inhalation, or ingestion, causing a spectrum of disease from localised abscesses to fulminant septicaemia with high mortality. *B. pseudomallei* has a large bipartite genome (~7.2 Mb across two chromosomes) with remarkable genomic plasticity, extensive horizontal gene transfer, and a diverse arsenal of virulence factors including type III and type VI secretion systems.
