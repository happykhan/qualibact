*Enterococcus raffinosus* is a Gram-positive coccus in the family *Enterococcaceae*, distinguished by its ability to ferment raffinose and other carbohydrates. It is an uncommon but emerging cause of healthcare-associated infections including bacteraemia, endocarditis, and urinary tract infections, particularly in immunocompromised patients. *E. raffinosus* isolates frequently carry intrinsic and acquired antimicrobial resistance determinants, including vancomycin resistance genes.
