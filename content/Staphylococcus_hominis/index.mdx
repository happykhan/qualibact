*Staphylococcus hominis* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, commonly found on human skin, particularly in areas with apocrine glands. It is an opportunistic pathogen implicated in bloodstream infections, prosthetic device infections, and peritonitis in peritoneal dialysis patients, with certain lineages displaying resistance to multiple antibiotics. Genomically, *S. hominis* harbours a moderately sized genome with mobile genetic elements carrying resistance and virulence determinants, and its population structure includes globally disseminated hospital-adapted clones.
