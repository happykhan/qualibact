*Klebsiella quasivariicola* is a Gram-negative, encapsulated, non-motile rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, closely related to *Klebsiella variicola* within the *K. pneumoniae* species complex. It has been isolated from both clinical and environmental sources but is often misclassified as *K. pneumoniae* or *K. variicola* by phenotypic methods. Whole-genome sequencing reliably distinguishes *K. quasivariicola* as a separate species, and isolates may carry capsular diversity loci and acquired antimicrobial resistance genes.
