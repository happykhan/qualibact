*Bacillus mobilis* is an aerobic, endospore-forming, motile Gram-positive rod in the family *Bacillaceae*, classified within the *Bacillus cereus* sensu lato group. It was recently delineated as a distinct genomospecies based on whole-genome analyses and has been isolated from diverse environments including soil, food, and clinical specimens. *B. mobilis* shares the core chromosomal architecture of the *B. cereus* group and may carry enterotoxin genes, though its clinical and ecological significance remains less well characterised than that of *B. cereus* or *B. thuringiensis*.
