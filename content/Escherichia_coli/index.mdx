*Escherichia coli* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, and one of the best-characterised model organisms in microbiology. While predominantly a harmless commensal of the human gastrointestinal tract, pathogenic variants cause a wide spectrum of diseases including urinary tract infections, neonatal meningitis, bacteraemia, and diarrhoeal syndromes mediated by distinct pathotypes (UPEC, EHEC, ETEC, EIEC, among others). *E. coli* has an open pangenome with extensive genomic plasticity driven by horizontal gene transfer, phages, and plasmids, and is a critical sentinel organism for antimicrobial resistance surveillance globally.
