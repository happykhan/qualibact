*Citrobacter farmeri* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod of the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, isolated from clinical specimens, food, and environmental sources. It is an uncommon but recognised opportunistic pathogen capable of causing urinary tract infections, bloodstream infections, and wound infections, particularly in debilitated patients. *C. farmeri* was formerly known as *Citrobacter amalonaticus* biogroup 1, and its genome encodes a variety of antimicrobial resistance determinants including chromosomal beta-lactamases.
