*Pseudomonas aeruginosa* is a Gram-negative, aerobic, non-fermenting rod in the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, ubiquitous in soil, water, and moist environments. It is one of the most clinically significant opportunistic pathogens, causing chronic lung infections in cystic fibrosis patients, ventilator-associated pneumonia, burn wound infections, and bloodstream infections, and is listed by the WHO as a critical-priority pathogen due to carbapenem resistance. *P. aeruginosa* possesses a large, highly adaptable genome (~6.3 Mb) encoding quorum-sensing networks, type III and type VI secretion systems, extensive efflux pumps, and a broad capacity for biofilm formation.
