*Clostridium septicum* is a Gram-positive, anaerobic, spore-forming rod of the family *Clostridiaceae*, found in soil and the gastrointestinal tract of humans and animals. It is a highly virulent pathogen that causes non-traumatic gas gangrene (spontaneous myonecrosis), often in association with underlying colorectal malignancy or immunosuppression, and produces the pore-forming alpha-toxin as its principal virulence factor. *C. septicum* has a genome of approximately 3.5 Mb and is aerotolerant relative to many clostridia, facilitating its ability to invade and destroy healthy tissue in the absence of prior traumatic injury.
