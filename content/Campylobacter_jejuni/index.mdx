*Campylobacter jejuni* is a Gram-negative, microaerophilic, spiral-shaped rod of the family *Campylobacteraceae* and the leading bacterial cause of foodborne gastroenteritis worldwide. It is commonly transmitted through undercooked poultry, unpasteurised milk, and contaminated water, causing acute diarrhoeal illness and, in rare cases, post-infectious Guillain-Barré syndrome. *C. jejuni* has a relatively small (~1.6 Mb), AT-rich genome with extensive phase-variable contingency loci, high natural competence for DNA uptake, and frequent interstrain recombination.
