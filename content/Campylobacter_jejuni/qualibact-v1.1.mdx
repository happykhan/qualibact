QualiBact `qualibact-v1.1` thresholds for *Campylobacter jejuni*, derived from a curated 654-genome ATB subset re-run with `qualibact-engine` for v1.1.

Compared with v1.0, the curated re-run pulls several bounds inward:

- **N50 lower** raised from 23 kb (v1.0) to **36 kb**.
- **Contig-count upper** tightened from 160 (v1.0) to **120**.
- **GC content** narrowed to a 30 – 31 % FINAL / WARN band (vs. 30.06 – 30.85 % FINAL / 30.13 – 30.53 % WARN in v1.0).

**Assembly-size upper bound pinned at 2.1 Mb** on advice from the *Campylobacter jejuni* subgroup (INSA Lisboa Campylobacter subgroup). The engine emitted 2.0 Mb for the curated run, but expert review of a diverse isolate set (human + food + animal + environmental) showed that 2.0 Mb was excluding a small number of well-supported, high-quality genomes; 2.1 Mb retains them.
