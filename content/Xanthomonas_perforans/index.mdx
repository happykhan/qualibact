*Xanthomonas perforans* is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped plant pathogen of the family *Xanthomonadaceae* and a primary cause of bacterial spot on tomato, particularly in warm and humid growing regions. Originally described as a distinct species from *X. euvesicatoria*, it has become the dominant bacterial spot species on tomato in many parts of the world, displacing other xanthomonads. *X. perforans* genomes carry a dynamic set of type III effectors, with frequent horizontal exchange and effector gene loss enabling rapid adaptation to host resistance.
