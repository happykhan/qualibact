*Bacteroides fragilis* is an obligately anaerobic, non-spore-forming Gram-negative rod in the family *Bacteroidaceae*, and the most clinically significant species within the genus. Despite being a minority member of the normal colonic microbiota, it is the anaerobe most frequently isolated from intra-abdominal abscesses, peritonitis, and bloodstream infections, with its polysaccharide capsule being a key virulence determinant that promotes abscess formation. *B. fragilis* has a genome of approximately 5.2 Mb with extensive phase-variable surface polysaccharide loci and, in enterotoxigenic strains, encodes the metalloprotease fragilysin (BFT), which has been linked to inflammatory diarrhoea and colorectal neoplasia.
