*Collinsella aerofaciens* is a Gram-positive, obligately anaerobic, non-spore-forming coccobacillus of the family *Coriobacteriaceae* within the phylum *Actinomycetota*, and one of the most abundant members of the adult human gut microbiota. It is a major producer of hydrogen gas through carbohydrate fermentation and has been associated with both health and disease states, including links to metabolic syndrome, atherosclerosis, and irritable bowel syndrome. *C. aerofaciens* has a relatively small genome (~2.4 Mb) with limited metabolic versatility compared to other gut anaerobes, reflecting its specialised niche as a fermentative commensal.
