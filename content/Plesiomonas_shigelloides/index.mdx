*Plesiomonas shigelloides* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod and the sole species in the genus *Plesiomonas*, currently placed in the family *Enterobacteriaceae* (order *Enterobacterales*). It inhabits freshwater and estuarine environments and causes acute gastroenteritis, particularly in tropical and subtropical regions, typically following consumption of raw shellfish or contaminated water. *P. shigelloides* genomes encode iron-acquisition systems, a type II and type VI secretion system, and an O-antigen that cross-reacts with *Shigella sonnei*, which historically complicated serological diagnosis.
