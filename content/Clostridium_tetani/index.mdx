*Clostridium tetani* is an anaerobic, Gram-positive, spore-forming rod of the family *Clostridiaceae* and the causative agent of tetanus, a life-threatening neurological disease characterised by spastic paralysis. Its highly resistant endospores are ubiquitous in soil and can contaminate wounds, where vegetative cells produce tetanospasmin, one of the most potent biological toxins known. The genome of *C. tetani* is relatively small (~2.8 Mb) with the tetanus toxin gene (*tetX*) carried on a large virulence plasmid (pE88).
