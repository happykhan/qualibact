*Porphyromonas gingivalis* is a Gram-negative, obligately anaerobic, non-motile rod in the family *Porphyromonadaceae*, and a keystone pathogen of chronic periodontitis. Despite being present at low abundance in subgingival plaque, it can dysregulate the host immune response and reshape the oral microbial community to promote inflammatory tissue destruction. The genome of *P. gingivalis* encodes potent cysteine proteases known as gingipains, fimbriae, and a capsule, and has been linked epidemiologically to systemic conditions including cardiovascular disease and Alzheimer's disease.
