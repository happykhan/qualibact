*Rickettsia rickettsii* is a Gram-negative, obligate intracellular bacterium in the family *Rickettsiaceae*, and the causative agent of Rocky Mountain spotted fever, the most severe rickettsial illness in the Americas. It is transmitted to humans through the bite of infected ixodid ticks, targets vascular endothelial cells, and can produce life-threatening vasculitis, coagulopathy, and multi-organ failure if untreated. *R. rickettsii* has a highly reduced genome (~1.3 Mb) with significant pseudogene content, reflecting its obligate parasitic lifestyle and dependence on host-cell metabolites.
