*Orientia tsutsugamushi* is an obligate intracellular, Gram-negative bacterium in the family *Rickettsiaceae* (order *Rickettsiales*), and the causative agent of scrub typhus, a mite-borne febrile illness endemic across the Asia-Pacific region and increasingly recognised in other tropical and subtropical areas. Infection occurs through the bite of infected larval trombiculid mites (chiggers) and can cause eschar formation, pneumonitis, meningoencephalitis, and multiorgan failure if untreated. The genome of *O. tsutsugamushi* (~2.0-2.1 Mb) is remarkable for massive amplification of conjugative transposon-like elements comprising over 40% of the sequence, making it one of the most repeat-dense bacterial genomes known.
