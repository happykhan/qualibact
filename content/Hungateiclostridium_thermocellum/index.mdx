*Hungateiclostridium thermocellum* (also classified as *Clostridium thermocellum* or *Acetivibrio thermocellus*) is an anaerobic, Gram-positive, thermophilic, spore-forming rod of the family *Oscillospiraceae*. It is the most well-studied cellulolytic thermophile and is of major biotechnological interest for consolidated bioprocessing of lignocellulosic biomass into ethanol and other biofuels. The genome of *H. thermocellum* encodes a large, elaborate cellulosome complex -- a multi-enzyme, cell-surface-attached assembly -- that enables highly efficient deconstruction of crystalline cellulose.
