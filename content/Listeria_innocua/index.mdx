*Listeria innocua* is a non-pathogenic, Gram-positive, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Listeriaceae*, commonly found in soil, decaying vegetation, and food-processing environments. It is the closest phylogenetic relative of the pathogen *L. monocytogenes* and is frequently used as a non-pathogenic surrogate in food-safety research. The genome of *L. innocua* (~3.0 Mb) lacks the major virulence gene cluster (LIPI-1) of *L. monocytogenes*, providing insight into the evolutionary gain and loss of pathogenicity within the genus.
