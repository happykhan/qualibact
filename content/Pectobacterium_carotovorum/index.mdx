*Pectobacterium carotovorum* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Pectobacteriaceae*, and one of the most economically important bacterial soft-rot pathogens worldwide. It has a broad host range, causing tissue maceration in a wide variety of crops including potato, carrot, and leafy vegetables, through the production of pectinases, cellulases, and proteases. *P. carotovorum* exhibits substantial subspecific genomic diversity, with extensive variation in virulence gene repertoires and regulatory networks across its multiple subspecies.
