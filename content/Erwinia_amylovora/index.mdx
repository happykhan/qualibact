*Erwinia amylovora* is a facultatively anaerobic, Gram-negative rod of the family *Erwiniaceae* and the causative agent of fire blight, a devastating disease of apple, pear, and other rosaceous plants. It was the first bacterium demonstrated to be a plant pathogen and remains one of the most destructive threats to pome fruit production worldwide. The genome of *E. amylovora* encodes key pathogenicity determinants including a type III secretion system, the exopolysaccharide amylovoran, and the small protein harpin (HrpN) that elicits the hypersensitive response in resistant hosts.
