*Bifidobacterium bifidum* is a Gram-positive, anaerobic, non-motile rod belonging to the family *Bifidobacteriaceae* within the phylum *Actinomycetota*. It is one of the earliest colonisers of the human infant gut and is considered a key commensal associated with immune modulation, pathogen exclusion, and mucin degradation. Genomically, *B. bifidum* possesses a relatively compact genome enriched in glycosyl hydrolases that enable the breakdown of host-derived glycans such as mucin O-glycans and human milk oligosaccharides.
