*Brachyspira hyodysenteriae* is an anaerobic, Gram-negative spirochaete of the family *Brachyspiraceae* and the causative agent of swine dysentery, a severe mucohaemorrhagic colitis in pigs. It colonises the large intestinal crypts and produces haemolysins that drive tissue damage, making it one of the most economically important enteric pathogens in pig production worldwide. The genome of *B. hyodysenteriae* is relatively small for a spirochaete and encodes multiple outer-membrane proteins and NADH oxidases thought to be key virulence determinants.
