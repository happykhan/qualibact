*Borreliella garinii* is a Gram-negative spirochaete of the family *Borreliaceae* and a major causative agent of Lyme borreliosis in Europe and Asia. It displays a strong neurotropism and is the genospecies most frequently associated with Lyme neuroborreliosis. Like other members of the *Borreliella burgdorferi* sensu lato complex, *B. garinii* carries a segmented genome with a linear chromosome and multiple linear and circular plasmids, and it shows high genetic diversity among outer surface protein genes involved in host and vector adaptation.
