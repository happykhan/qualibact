*Bordetella pertussis* is a Gram-negative, aerobic coccobacillus of the family *Alcaligenaceae* and the causative agent of whooping cough (pertussis) in humans. It is a strictly human-adapted pathogen that colonises the ciliated respiratory epithelium and produces a suite of virulence factors, including pertussis toxin, filamentous haemagglutinin, and adenylate cyclase toxin. *B. pertussis* has a highly clonal population structure with a relatively small, IS-element-rich genome that arose through reductive evolution from a *Bordetella bronchiseptica*-like ancestor.
