*Bradyrhizobium diazoefficiens* is a slow-growing, Gram-negative alphaproteobacterium of the family *Bradyrhizobiaceae*, best known for its ability to form nitrogen-fixing root nodule symbioses with soybean and other legumes. It is one of the most agronomically important rhizobia and is widely used as a crop inoculant to enhance biological nitrogen fixation. *B. diazoefficiens* possesses a large genome (approximately 9.1 Mb) with extensive regulatory networks governing symbiosis, denitrification, and phototrophy.
