*Burkholderia contaminans* is a non-fermenting, Gram-negative rod belonging to the *Burkholderia cepacia* complex (Bcc) within the order *Burkholderiales*. It has been recovered from pharmaceutical and industrial products as a contaminant and is also recognised as an opportunistic pathogen, particularly in people with cystic fibrosis and chronic granulomatous disease. Like other Bcc members, *B. contaminans* carries a multi-replicon genome with substantial genomic plasticity and intrinsic resistance to many antimicrobial agents.
