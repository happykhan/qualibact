*Staphylococcus lugdunensis* is a coagulase-negative, Gram-positive coccus of the family *Staphylococcaceae*, found as a commensal on human skin, especially of the inguinal and perineal regions. Despite being coagulase-negative, it behaves clinically like *S. aureus*, causing aggressive native-valve endocarditis, skin abscesses, and bone and joint infections with high morbidity. Genomically, *S. lugdunensis* is notable for a relatively conserved genome, production of the bacteriocin lugdunin, and retention of broad antibiotic susceptibility compared with other coagulase-negative staphylococci.
