*Clostridioides difficile* (formerly *Clostridium difficile*) is a Gram-positive, anaerobic, spore-forming rod of the family *Peptostreptococcaceae*, and the leading cause of antibiotic-associated diarrhoea and pseudomembranous colitis in healthcare settings worldwide. Disease is primarily mediated by two large clostridial toxins (TcdA and TcdB), with some hypervirulent strains also producing the binary toxin CDT. *C. difficile* has a mosaic genome of approximately 4.3 Mb with extensive mobile genetic element content, high rates of horizontal gene transfer, and notable genomic diversity between epidemic ribotypes.
