*Corynebacterium striatum* is a Gram-positive, non-spore-forming, club-shaped rod of the family *Corynebacteriaceae*, normally found as a commensal on human skin and mucous membranes. It has emerged as a multidrug-resistant opportunistic pathogen causing healthcare-associated infections including bloodstream infections, prosthetic joint infections, endocarditis, and respiratory tract infections. *C. striatum* genomes carry diverse antimicrobial resistance genes, often on mobile genetic elements, and the species displays considerable clonal diversity with evidence of nosocomial transmission clusters.
