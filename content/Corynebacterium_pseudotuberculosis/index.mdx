*Corynebacterium pseudotuberculosis* is a Gram-positive, non-spore-forming, club-shaped rod of the family *Corynebacteriaceae*, and the causative agent of caseous lymphadenitis (CLA) in sheep and goats, as well as ulcerative lymphangitis in horses. It is a facultative intracellular pathogen that produces phospholipase D, a key virulence exotoxin that promotes dissemination within the host, and can occasionally cause zoonotic lymphadenitis in people with occupational livestock exposure. *C. pseudotuberculosis* has a compact genome of approximately 2.3 Mb with two recognised biovars (ovis and equi), and strains may carry the diphtheria toxin gene on a lysogenic corynephage.
