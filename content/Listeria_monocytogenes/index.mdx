*Listeria monocytogenes* is a Gram-positive, facultatively anaerobic, intracellular rod in the family *Listeriaceae*, and the causative agent of listeriosis, a severe foodborne infection with high case-fatality rates in pregnant women, neonates, the elderly, and immunocompromised individuals. It is ubiquitous in soil and water and can persist in food-processing facilities owing to its tolerance of cold, salt, and low pH. The genome of *L. monocytogenes* (~2.9 Mb) encodes key virulence factors such as listeriolysin O, ActA, and internalins, with four well-defined phylogenetic lineages showing distinct ecological and pathogenic profiles.
