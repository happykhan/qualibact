*Streptococcus anginosus* is a Gram-positive coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae* and a member of the *Streptococcus anginosus* group (formerly *S. milleri*), typically exhibiting variable haemolysis. It is a commensal of the oral cavity, gastrointestinal tract, and urogenital tract, but is notable for its strong tendency to cause pyogenic abscesses in the brain, liver, and other deep-seated sites. Genomically, *S. anginosus* possesses a relatively small streptococcal genome with virulence factors including intermedilysin and genes facilitating abscess formation and tissue invasion.
