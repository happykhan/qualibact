*Burkholderia gladioli* is a Gram-negative, non-fermenting rod of the family *Burkholderiaceae*, originally isolated as a plant pathogen causing gladiolus diseases. It has emerged as an opportunistic pathogen in individuals with cystic fibrosis and chronic granulomatous disease, capable of causing respiratory infections that can be difficult to distinguish clinically from *B. cepacia* complex infections. *B. gladioli* carries a large multi-replicon genome encoding diverse secondary metabolite biosynthesis clusters and inherent resistance to multiple antibiotic classes.
