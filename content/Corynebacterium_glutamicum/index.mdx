*Corynebacterium glutamicum* is a non-pathogenic, Gram-positive, non-sporulating rod of the family *Corynebacteriaceae* within the order *Mycobacteriales*. It is one of the most industrially important bacteria, used globally for the large-scale production of amino acids, particularly L-glutamate and L-lysine, with annual outputs exceeding millions of tonnes. The well-characterised 3.3 Mb genome of *C. glutamicum* has made it a primary platform organism for metabolic engineering and systems biology of the *Corynebacterium*-*Mycobacterium*-*Nocardia* (CMN) clade.
