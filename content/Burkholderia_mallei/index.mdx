*Burkholderia mallei* is a non-motile, Gram-negative rod of the family *Burkholderiaceae* and the causative agent of glanders, an ancient and often fatal zoonotic disease primarily affecting equids. It is an obligate mammalian pathogen with no known environmental reservoir and is classified as a CDC Tier 1 select agent due to its bioweapon potential. *B. mallei* evolved by genome reduction from *B. pseudomallei*, retaining a two-chromosome structure but having lost approximately 1 Mb of DNA, including flagellar genes and many metabolic pathways.
