*Streptococcus thermophilus* is a Gram-positive, alpha-haemolytic, thermophilic coccus of the family *Streptococcaceae*, and the only streptococcal species with Generally Recognised As Safe (GRAS) status. It is widely used as a starter culture in the dairy industry for the production of yoghurt and certain cheeses, where it rapidly ferments lactose and produces exopolysaccharides that contribute to texture. Genomically, *S. thermophilus* has undergone extensive gene loss and decay compared with pathogenic streptococci, with a reduced genome reflecting adaptation to the nutrient-rich milk environment.
