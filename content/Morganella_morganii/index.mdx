*Morganella morganii* is a motile, Gram-negative rod in the family *Morganellaceae* (order *Enterobacterales*), found in the environment and as part of the normal intestinal flora of humans and reptiles. It is an opportunistic pathogen associated with urinary tract infections, wound infections, bacteraemia, and nosocomial outbreaks, often in debilitated or postoperative patients. The genome of *M. morganii* (~3.8 Mb) frequently carries chromosomal AmpC beta-lactamase and has increasing prevalence of plasmid-mediated carbapenemase and extended-spectrum beta-lactamase genes.
