*Pluralibacter gergoviae* is a Gram-negative, facultatively anaerobic rod in the family *Enterobacteriaceae*, formerly classified within *Enterobacter*, and found in environmental sources including water, soil, and cosmetic products. It is an uncommon but emerging opportunistic pathogen associated with nosocomial urinary tract infections, bloodstream infections, and respiratory infections, particularly in immunocompromised patients. *P. gergoviae* is notable for its intrinsic resistance to colistin and frequently carries extended-spectrum beta-lactamase and carbapenemase genes on mobile genetic elements.
