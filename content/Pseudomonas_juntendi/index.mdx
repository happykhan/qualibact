*Pseudomonas juntendi* is a non-fermenting Gram-negative rod of the family *Pseudomonadaceae*, first described from clinical specimens in Japan. It is closely related to *Pseudomonas aeruginosa* and has been isolated from blood, respiratory secretions, and urine of hospitalised patients, suggesting a role as an opportunistic pathogen. *P. juntendi* is differentiated from *P. aeruginosa* by multilocus sequence analysis and whole-genome comparisons, and may carry clinically relevant antimicrobial resistance determinants.
