*Mycobacterium smegmatis* is a Gram-positive (acid-fast), rapidly growing, non-pathogenic mycobacterium in the family *Mycobacteriaceae*, commonly found in soil and water. It serves as the principal laboratory model organism for studying mycobacterial biology, including cell wall biosynthesis, drug resistance mechanisms, and the genetics of *Mycobacterium tuberculosis*. *M. smegmatis* has a relatively large mycobacterial genome (~7 Mb) with a high GC content and a diverse repertoire of lipid metabolism and secondary metabolite genes.
