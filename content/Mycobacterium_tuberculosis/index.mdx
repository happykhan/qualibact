*Mycobacterium tuberculosis* is a slow-growing, aerobic, acid-fast bacillus in the family *Mycobacteriaceae*, and the causative agent of tuberculosis, which remains the leading cause of death from a single infectious agent worldwide. It is an obligate human pathogen transmitted via respiratory aerosols, capable of establishing latent infection that can persist for decades before reactivating. The genome of *M. tuberculosis* (~4.4 Mb) is remarkably conserved with very limited horizontal gene transfer, and its GC-rich sequence encodes an unusually large number of genes devoted to lipid metabolism and cell-wall biosynthesis, key determinants of its pathogenicity and drug resistance.
