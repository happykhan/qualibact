*Kocuria rhizophila* is a Gram-positive, aerobic coccus in the family *Micrococcaceae* within the order *Micrococcales*, commonly found in soil, the rhizosphere, and on human skin. Although generally considered a low-virulence commensal, it has been reported as a cause of catheter-related bloodstream infections, peritonitis, and endocarditis in immunocompromised patients. *K. rhizophila* has a relatively compact genome and is widely used as a quality-control organism in antimicrobial susceptibility testing.
